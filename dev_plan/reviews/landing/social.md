## Top 8, ranked

1. **No social launch until "Take office" and the Daily link work.** Every post must end in a playable link.
2. **15 s vertical hook, built natively, not cropped.** On phones the wall's game tiles are unreadable desktop UI. Stack a REAL card (date, one line, pin drop) over one phone-legible crisis card, then Execute → Chronicle headline. Use Taiwan drills → "The first shot". Captions burned in, tags clear of the bottom 20% TikTok UI.
3. **Swap the OG card** to "The free economic zone" or "The first shot" (state dilemmas, not repression) and enlarge it to ~40% width.
4. **Fix the share result:** one verdict, stars that match the grade, "Daily #12" on local date (currently UTC), three lines max (`World Conflict Daily #12 🇹🇼 ★★☆` / emoji strip / link). Drop the real-leader archetype line ("Mohamed bin Zayed, Cautious Caretaker"): that is what gets quote-tweeted.
5. **"Real vs Game" carousel**, 6 slides, closing on a "What really happened" debrief. Leave out Sudan and Kashmir.
6. **Four looping GIFs (<6 s):** Execute → Chronicle; coup plot → red heartbeat; layer cycle with confidence ranges; stars stamping in.
7. **30 s trailer:** gameplay by second 3, three pairs (Taiwan, Hormuz, Europe strikes), end-screen stamp, disclaimer. Never open on a death.
8. **Streamer kit:** a "chaos seed" tuned for coups and late consequences, a stream-length Term, and the LLM tier so chat watches a rival "think". Seeing the coup coming and falling anyway is the clip.

Framing rules: lead with the system ("everyone plays from bad intel"), never a death or a leader's name in a title; pin the disclaimer; pause Iran/Ukraine posts 48 h after mass-casualty news.
