# Landing page review: brand and PR

Reviewed at 1440×900 and 390×844. No broken assets or horizontal scroll.

## Scorecard

| Area | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | 6 | "Real crises, your desk" is clear; the actual edge (15-minute sessions, everyone plays from bad intel, "what really happened" debrief) is scattered and never set against comparables. |
| Tone | 6 | Restrained copy, no casualty stakes, no gunfire. But the first frame is a 90px headline "US–Israeli strikes kill Iran's Supreme Leader" with a pulsing red target pin, before the logo, tagline or any framing. |
| Risk handling | 6 | Fact/fiction labelling per card is exemplary. The disclaimer is footer-only (13px grey); the page hides the Assassinate and nuclear orders journalists will find. |
| Press readiness | 3 | No pitch line, boilerplate, contact, press kit, signup or repo link. "Coming soon" is a dead end. |
| **Overall** | **5.5** | Careful foundations, launch-blocking gaps. |

## Top 8 changes (ranked)

1. **Open on the game, not the death.** Start the intro wall on Ukraine or Taiwan, or show the title and tagline over the wall for the first 3 s. Remove the pulsing ring from the Tehran pin (a static dot reads as a locator; a pulsing ring reads as a hit marker).
2. **Put the disclaimer above the fold.** Under the hero CTA: *"Real events up to October 2026. Everything after is fiction: in-game words and decisions of real leaders are invented."* Repeat one line under the parallels timeline. Keep the full footer text.
3. **Add a capture point.** Replace the inert "Coming soon" with *"Get the launch email"* (single field) plus "Follow development" (set `repoUrl`). Every press hit currently leaks.
4. **State the angle against comparables** in the hook: *"Paradox depth in a 15-minute session. Suzerain's hard choices, on a live map of 2026. Every government, including yours, plays from half-wrong intelligence."* (Press and Show HN only, if naming brands on the page worries you.)
5. **Fix factual and editorial slips.** "You read these headlines last year" is wrong (Tehran was February 2026, Oleshky last week): use *"You read these headlines this year."* "A dictator in a New York courtroom" is an editorial label and overstates a trial: use *"A deposed president facing charges in New York."* "The world as it stands today" will date in weeks: use *"the world as it stood in November 2026."*
6. **Pre-empt the assassination question.** Add to "Built on the real world": *"Covert action, assassinations and nuclear weapons exist in the game because they exist in the world. They are costly, rarely work, and the world remembers. You are never asked to target a real person by name."* Verify that last clause against the UI before publishing; if it is false, change the game (target the office, not the person) rather than the copy.
7. **Ship /press:** one-liner (*"World Conflict is a 15-minute geopolitical strategy game set in November 2026, where every nation acts on its own history and half-wrong intelligence."*), 50/150-word boilerplate, fact sheet (in development, not publicly playable), real-events statement, screenshots, logo, contact.
8. **Add a borders and content note** in the footer: *"Map borders follow Natural Earth and imply no position on disputed territory, including Kashmir, Taiwan and Crimea. Contains depictions of war, coups and political violence."*

## Questions journalists will ask

Can I kill a real leader, or nuke a city? Why open on Khamenei? Who decides what "really happened"? How are Palestine and Gaza handled? Do LLMs invent leaders' words? When can I play?

## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| "Game lets you assassinate [leader]" headline | High | Change 6; target offices, not names. |
| Opening on a real killing read as glorifying | Medium | Change 1. |
| China objection to playable Taiwan | High in CN | Accept no CN release; borders note. |
| India map law (Kashmir) | Medium | Localise or exclude; legal review. |
| Publicity / false endorsement | Low | No faces, quotes or leaders in ad copy. |
| LLM text defaming real leaders | Medium | Label as simulated; never use it in marketing. |
| Real events overtake a flashpoint | Medium | Pause plan; "facts as of" date per card. |
| Claims ("No account", "Daily") while unplayable | Medium | "In development" beside each; add a launch window. |
| Store content review (violence, AI) | Medium | Honest survey, AI disclosure, store disclaimer. |
| Copyrighted lyric in README | Low | Remove before linking the repo. |
