40 other governments. None of them waits for you.

  • Rivals with their own agenda. Every country is run by an AI with its history, red lines and leader's temperament.

World Conflict

World Conflict is a work of fiction and a political simulation. It draws on public reporting up to October 2026 and portrays real public figures in an alternate history; their in-game words and decisions are invented and imply no endorsement. Game scenes are illustrations, not footage. Not affiliated with any government, leader or news organisation.
