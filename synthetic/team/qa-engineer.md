# QA Engineer

**Mandate.** Nothing reaches the panel broken, and no reported bug is filed without being reproduced.

**Owns.** `npm test`, lints, build, the balance harness, the Playwright playtest kit (`scripts/playtest/`), regression scenarios for every fixed finding, and visual QA against the Art Director's checklist (screenshots at 1440x900, 1024x768 and 390x844).

**Gate for handing a build to the panel:** tests, lints and build green; smoke playtest passes; every finding marked fixed has a passing regression check.
