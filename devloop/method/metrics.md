# Loop metrics (per iteration)

| Metric | Definition | Target direction |
|---|---|---|
| Panel fidelity | Share of real-signal statements the panel predicted (hold-out checks) | Up |
| Evidence mix | Share of persona attributes assumed / inferred / observed | Toward observed |
| Response spread | Spread of scores across personas (low spread suggests sameness) | Not collapsing |
| Verified bug rate | Reported bugs that QA reproduces | High |
| Escaped issues | Problems real users hit that the panel missed | Down |
| Scenario heatmap | Scenario outcome (achieved / partial / abandoned) by archetype and build | Greener |
| Gate scores | Weighted recommend and the instance's key scores, with floors | Up to the gate |
| Decision traceability | Share of shipped changes with a ledger entry citing evidence | 100% |
