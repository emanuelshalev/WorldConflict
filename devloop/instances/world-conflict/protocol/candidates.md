# Candidate improvements for the forced trade-off (interview step 3)

Phrased as player-facing outcomes, not implementation. Spread 100 points; cut the two you care least about.

| ID | Candidate |
|---|---|
| C1 | When I can't do something, I see why before I try, and what I need to do instead. |
| C2 | Warnings point at the thing they mean on the screen (the meter, the button). |
| C3 | Each month, the game tells me clearly what my move is and what my goal needs. |
| C4 | My advisors' suggestions are always visible, explain themselves, and I can accept them with one tap. |
| C5 | Whatever I can do from the menus I can also do by tapping a country on the map, the same way. |
| C6 | The game looks and feels like a crafted game: illustrated cards, animation and sound when I act, not a dashboard of buttons. |
| C7 | Consistent names for everything (the same word for the same resource everywhere). |
| C8 | Bigger, more dramatic moments (wars, coups, nukes) with full-screen scenes. |
| C9 | More depth and more systems to master over long campaigns. |
| C10 | More accurate real-world content and richer debriefs about what really happened. |
| C11 | A shorter, faster month: fewer screens between my decision and the result. |
| C12 | A better share card and replay highlights to post. |
