# Concept (neutral description for interviews)

World Conflict is a turn-based strategy game you play in a browser, on desktop or phone. You lead one of 41 real countries in the world of November 2026: current leaders, current wars and crises. Every other government is run by a computer opponent with its own goals and imperfect intelligence about you.

Each turn is one month. You face crises (choose one of two or three responses), give a few orders (diplomacy, military, intelligence, home politics) limited by how much political capital you have, then end the month and read what happened. There are three ways to play: a 12-month Flashpoint built around one real crisis with a clear goal (about 15 minutes), a 48-month term that ends in an election, and a 10-year Legacy. At the end you get a grade and a shareable card.
