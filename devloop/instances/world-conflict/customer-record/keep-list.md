# Keep list

Written by the Customer Advocate on 2026-11-24, from customer rounds 0 to 4 (iterations 0 to 4) and the CEO's real signals RS-001 to RS-008. It records what customers like, so the team can preserve it on purpose and predict what they will like next. Rounds 0 to 4 asked for positives only loosely (no "would miss" answers, three to five liked items per customer per round), so the evidence is thinner than it will be from round 5.

How to read it:
- Weight is the panel weight of the customers who liked the row, each counted once (Maya .18, Olivia .13, Ruth .13, Noah .10, Priya .10, Dan .10, Sam .09, Leo .09, Marcus .08; total 1.00). Rows are ordered by weight, strongest first.
- Strength: **strong** = a customer calls it the best part or a reason to recommend or continue; **medium** = named in several rounds with warm words; **light** = named, no sign it moves a decision.
- State in round 4: **holds** (liked again, no new complaint that undoes it), **weakened** (liked before, now a complaint about it), **lost** (gone), **not seen in round 4** (not mentioned; this is not the same as lost).
- Entry rule (method): two or more customers, or one customer in two rounds, or a real person. The CEO's real signals outrank the panel. Where the CEO named a thing as good, the row says so; where the CEO only demanded it, the row says "demand".
- Round 4 was played on two builds (2083597 and 876ea58) and 4 of 9 customers reached an end screen, so rows about the ending rest on fewer voices.

- **Since-which-round is the first round it appears in the record**, not when it was first on screen. K-12 (memory) may be older than round 4.
