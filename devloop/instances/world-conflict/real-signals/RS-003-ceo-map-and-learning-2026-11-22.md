# RS-003: CEO on the world map and learning while playing (2026-11-22)

## Verbatim (condensed)
> The world map should zoom to the screen; it would be great to have country names on the globe so it's richer. When changing eras while choosing the game mode, the map stays the same, while stability and significant situations (with icons and lines) should be different and are not represented.
> The explanations may contain names and concepts people are not familiar with. We want this game to be a learning tool as well, so we need a way to easily access explanations of terms and situations: maybe tooltip style while hovering on words, or an "i" icon for a more in-depth explanation of a paragraph or phrase. Players are not expected to know all the locations, leaders and situations, but we want to make playing an unknown situation accessible to all and let everyone familiarize themselves with the information. It is a fun game, but also educational.
> Some players will like to play familiar situations, so we don't want to bother them with accessibility UI elements they don't need. Think about what can be done, collect user feedback, find the best UI, maybe a way to turn these features on and off. The goals are clear.

## Mapping to archetypes
- Ruth (67, non-gamer, follows news but not every name), Noah (16, knows little geopolitics), Maya and Olivia (casual): need explanations in place.
- Priya and Marcus (know the material): want it out of the way; Marcus wants era maps that are right.
- Sam: judges whether learning aids are crafted or clutter.

## Gap and correction
- The panel did not raise unfamiliar names as a blocker in iteration 0, although Ruth noted abbreviations and Priya asked for real-world context. The panel reads with the knowledge of a language model: a known bias (synthetic respondents know too much). **Correction:** add to every persona a `knowledge` attribute (what they do and do not know about current affairs) with evidence `observed / RS-003`, and instruct customers to flag every name or concept their persona would not know.
- New goals G11 (a world map that fits and tells the era's story) and G12 (learn as you play, adjustable). New interview candidates C14, C15.
