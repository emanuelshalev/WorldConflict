# RS-006: CEO playtest (2026-11-23), the desk has no clear flow

> It's unclear what the things to go through are: some things on the left panel, some things on the right. There is no clear way to iterate over the things to do or open, and to be intentional about what I want to skip. What should I do, go over all items on the left, then all items on the right, and add customization as needed? Also it's not intuitively clear what is clickable and what is not.

## Mapping
Every archetype, most strongly Ruth and Olivia (no gaming conventions), Maya (wants one obvious next step), Sam (affordance and hierarchy craft), Dan (wants to be deliberate about each item). Related: RS-001 (unclear what is expected), F-00-3/F-01-4 (density), RS-005 (staff picks unseen).

## Gap and correction
Panel personas followed the coach and the staff picks and rarely asked "what is my list of things to do this month?"; they also reported clickability problems only as retries attributed to the driver. **Correction:** personas must describe, at the start of each month, what they think their to-do list is and how they will get through it, and report every element they tried to click that did nothing (and every clickable thing they did not recognise as clickable) as friction of kind "affordance".
