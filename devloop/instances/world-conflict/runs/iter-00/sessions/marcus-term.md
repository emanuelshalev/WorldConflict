# Marcus, Term 2026, Ukraine, 1366x768

I wanted to replay one crisis twice with opposite choices and understand the difference. Partly got it.

**What I did.** One Term, 2026, Ukraine (tapped from the globe, the nation list on this screen shows one row only: devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/004-term3.png). Run A: I accepted every staff pick for 9 months. Run B: a fresh game, opposite choice on the Gaza vote, then staff defaults to August 2027.

**The good.** The reaction panel after a crisis is what I came for. Voting yes on the Gaza resolution: Israel 10 to 6, Egypt and Saudi Arabia +3 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/009-m1.png). Voting no: Israel 10 to 15, Egypt -4, Saudi -4 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/057-B2r.png). Same mechanism, mirrored, easy to follow. Choices show arrows for consequences before I commit (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/053-B2.png). The world is current: the embezzlement scandal around the inner circle, Hormuz, an oil shock after Iran.

**The bad.** I cannot run a clean experiment. The second game opened with a different crisis (Hostages in Belarus, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/050-B1.png), Gaza came a month later, and there is no seed, no rewind, no "restart this scenario". By August 2027 run A had approval 45, stability 33, score 81, GDP -14.8% (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/047-A12-desk.png); run B was at approval 61, stability 51, score 85 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/071-B9-a.png). Maybe my choices; maybe the dice; the game does not tell me. That is exactly my deal-breaker, "outcomes that feel unexplained", at the level of the whole run.

Smaller things: the crisis card says approval 59 to 57 while the month tile says -5.3 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/051-B1-a.png), so part of the change is unexplained. **Friction.** After the first few months I was approving the same staff suggestions (Reform program, ceasefire call to Putin) and pressing Execute (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/marcus-term/060-B4-desk.png).
