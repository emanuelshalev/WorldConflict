# Olivia, laptop (iteration 0, build b271008)

I clicked a friend's link and got a spinning globe with glowing pins and "Pick a fire. Put it out, or don't." That is a good first screen (shots/olivia-laptop/001-title.png). I pressed the big orange "Take office as Saudi Arabia" button because it was the only thing that looked like a start.

Then I hit a dark screen with a paragraph, and the same sentence twice, then a mission box with another paragraph (002-after-start.png). I do not read that. I pressed Take office just to get past it. First hesitation.

The desk (003-desk1.png) looked like work. Three columns, a list of meetings, little dots for capital, a tip banner, two buttons at the bottom, tiny grey text. I picked the middle card, Back channel, because it said "Staff pick" and sounded like making peace. Then Execute month.

That was the best moment: a result page with a character quote, a green bar going from -100 to -90, a world tension number going down (004-exec1.png). Something happened because of me, at about two minutes. I liked the newspaper front page too (008-deb-p4.png). The top tabs did not react when I tapped Diplomacy once; the bottom button did.

Month 2 asked me to vote on Iran sanctions. I did not know what "Abstain" would cost me, so I trusted the staff pick, pressed "Approve 4 suggestions", then Execute month (012-m2-approved.png). The page froze. Nothing moved, no message. I waited, then reloaded, and the tab never came back. I would have closed it right there, about five minutes in.

Where I did not feel blocked: nothing refused me. Where I felt stupid: the desk, a bit.
