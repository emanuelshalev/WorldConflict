Honest caveat: the machine was under crushing load (load average ~110), so every action took 30 to 60 seconds and I only got through month 1 of 10. Some slowness is not the game's fault, but on stream that is dead air all the same.

Good: Declare war, Nuclear strike, Assassinate, Back a coup are all right there. Searching "nuke india" found nothing, but "declare war on india" worked nicely (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/leo-stream/017-s5.png).

Not funny, barely visible. I removed the staff orders to get 3 capital, tried again, and the war never queued. The toast stayed on screen forever (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/leo-stream/028-w10.png). That is where I would have quit: I tried a wild move twice and got nothing.

But half the page is empty, no stinger, no sound, nothing I could clip. The newspaper, though, is lovely and postable (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/leo-stream/034-e3.png).

**Scores:** fun 5, visual 7, agency 4, recommend 5.
