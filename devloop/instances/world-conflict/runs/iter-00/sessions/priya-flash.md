# Priya (priya-flash, laptop), iteration 0

I played Ceasefire or Collapse as Zelensky, six months of twelve, then the page died.

**What worked.** The title globe pins all eight live crises, and I picked Ukraine in two clicks (shots/priya-flash/001-title.png, 004-pick3.png). The leaders are right: Zelensky, Putin, Macron, Merz, Takaichi, Frederiksen. UK PM "Andy Burnham" I could not verify and would look up before trusting the game. The briefing text is sharp ("Washington pushing a map across the table"; Patriots versus oil above $100, 030-a5.png). Best of all, every crisis option shows its effects with arrows and who backs it before I commit (007-desk.png). That is my whole wish: I understand what a choice is meant to do. The Putin call with a quote and a gauge felt like diplomacy (008-putin.png, 014-d1.png). I never hit an opaque refusal.

**What did not.**
- No real-world context. I came to learn what really happened. Debriefs and the Chronicle only restate my choice: "On blackout winter, President Zelensky decided: Ration and appeal to Europe" (036-us.png). The attached quote, "We are losing control of several districts", does not fit a power cut (032-deb1.png). Same boardroom picture every month.
- The "Home and security" page repeats the same Reform program card and the same two bullets each month (035-q3.png). By month 4 I skipped it.
- Numbers contradict themselves: stability 51.1 to 51.1 labelled +6; front 47 to 47 labelled +1 (021-p3.png); stability -8 on the crisis card but -2.9 on the month tile (032-deb1.png). I did not know which to believe.
- Staff pre-queue "Court United Kingdom" and "Reform program" every month and "Approve all" makes the month a button press. I chose crises, not orders, from month 3 on (007-desk.png).
- Clicking an option often did not register on the first try (030-a5.png).

**Quit point.** Month 7: Execute month stopped responding, then even reload timed out. The machine was heavily loaded (the browser GPU process was pegged), so this may be animation cost on a weak machine, but a student's laptop is weaker than this one. Before that I was already bored with the repeating debrief page.

**Scores.** Fun 6, clarity 7, visual 7, feels like a game 6, agency 7, realism 6, replay 5, recommend 5. I would not tell my Model UN club yet. Fix the real-world debriefs and I would.

**Note for the team.** Another persona overwrote my helper script in the shared scratchpad for a while, so a few of my commands may have hit another persona's driver (port 7806) and a few of theirs mine. My own driver log shows no foreign actions apart from that. Treat the Month 3 to 5 steps with a little care.
