# Ruth, tablet (build b271008)

I wanted to try being in charge during the Ukraine ceasefire talks. I did not get far, and I should say so plainly.

**What I did.** I paged the title screen to "Ceasefire or Collapse" and took office as President Trump. The opening card was good: my name, my mission in two sentences, "a crisis is already on your desk" (shots/ruth-tablet/003-g1.png). I chose the staff pick, pressed Execute month, and read the month page and the newspaper. Month two the same. In month three I opened the Mission chip (clear list of goals, 018-mis3.png) and tapped Map to find Ukraine. The page stopped answering. Every tap and even reloading timed out. I restarted the browser and my game was gone. I started a Term as Zelensky, played one month, and it froze again after the second Execute.

**Honest caveat.** The computer was very busy (load about 110 with many browsers), so the freezes may be that. I cannot tell. As Ruth I would simply have closed it.

**What I liked.**
- Real people and real places, and a proper newspaper.
- The month page showing "United States' view of you 45 to 49" with a quote from Trump (011-uc2.png). That is what I came for.

**What confused me.**
- The top bar says APPR, STAB, LEGIT, CAP, TENS in tiny letters (004-g2.png). I had to guess.
- "Approve 4", "1 left", "Orders 2/6", "In person 0/3": nobody explains these. After "Approve staff pick" the card still said "Your call", so I did not know whether I had decided (011-m2.png).
- The Mission chip and Map button did not respond to a normal tap by label.
- The title screen is crowded, and the list of nations in a Term is a two-row window; my tap on Ukraine missed after scrolling (006-term4.png).
- The newspaper put a North Korea missile story under a peace-talks picture with a Putin quote (009-p1.png). A history teacher notices.
- My goal was to end the Ukraine war, but the first months went to hostages in Afghanistan and a corruption scandal. Nothing told me which move helps Ukraine.

**Scores.** Fun 5, clarity 5, visual 7, feels like a game 6, agency 4, realism 6, replay 4, recommend 4. 

**Quit point.** Month three, tapping Map, the screen stopped answering.

**Wish.** Tell me in plain words, each month, how the Ukraine war moved because of what I did, and what really happened.
