# Sam, desktop 1920x1080 (build b271008)

Verdict after five minutes: not an AI dashboard on the surface, but you can still see the seams.

**What won me.** The title screen (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/001-title.png) has a serif wordmark, a globe as the menu and one line of voice, "Put it out, or don't." The globe map (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/002-map.png) with relation colours, pins and dotted lines is the first thing that feels designed. The debrief (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/012-d3b.png) has illustrated scenes and quoted replies from the other leader. The newspaper is a real object. I stopped assuming slop here.

**What cost me.**
- Desk (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/003-desk.png, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/007-desk2.png): the crisis picture is a 443px block with nothing beside it, the lower half of the centre column is empty, and the layout jumps when the tutorial banner closes. It reads as three cards of text. Frostpunk's decision screens use the whole stage.
- Capital: header diamonds go empty at 0 left while the rail's pips go filled. "Capital", "Orders", "In person", "Leadership score" vs "Score": one name per concept, please.
- Map to desk parity (S2): the Ceasefire queued from the map and appeared on the desk, good. But my first press did nothing visible, the menu closes with no motion or sound, and the map menu hides the 4% chance the desk shows. Disabled Destabilize explains itself only in a native tooltip (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/004-disabled.png). Into the Breach puts the reason on the tile.
- Debrief contradicts itself (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/012-d3b.png): a green check, a handshake and "ends the call on warmer terms" over a red "Propose ceasefire to Yemen (failed)". Approval is -6 in one card and -9.0 in the tiles (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/009-after.png).
- Generated prose: "Salman on After the Riyadh airport strike: Back channel via Muscat", then a lede that begins "On after the Riyadh airport strike" with a drop cap O and a stray n, over a fighter-jet picture for a diplomatic choice (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/014-d5.png). "The front moves l in your favour" (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/sam-desktop/013-d4.png). That is the tell.
- Map labels collide with pins around the UK, Lebanon and Afghanistan.
- The page froze twice with 30 s screenshot timeouts; the machine was heavily loaded, so I will not blame the game, but the globe is heavy.

**What I did not test.** I did one full month and part of a second. Staff pre-fills everything, so I made one real choice. I did not test sound, hover states beyond one, or the long game.

**Scores.** Visual 7, feels like a game 6, clarity 6, recommend 6.

**Biggest wish.** Make a button press feel like something (motion, sound, a stamp in the queue) and fix the places where the art and the status contradict what I chose.
