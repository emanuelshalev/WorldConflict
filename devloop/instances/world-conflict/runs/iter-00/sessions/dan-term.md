# Dan, dan-term (desktop, Pakistan, One Term, Normal), build b271008

I played 12 of 48 months. Honest summary: strong first hour, then I noticed I was just pressing Approve and Execute.

**Start.** The globe title is lovely (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/001-title.png). One Term, Pakistan (three red dots), done in four taps. The desk (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/006-desk1.png) gave me crises with signed effect arrows and who backs each option. That is real information.

**What I liked.** The All orders catalogue with typed search and previews: "nuclear strike india" showed 53% success and an 8% defence cost (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/015-nukeIndia.png). The month debrief Table, with a "Why your numbers moved" row and before/after numbers (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/018-table.png), is exactly what I want from this genre.

**What lost me.**
1. Refusals. Dimmed orders (strike nuclear sites, declare war) do nothing on click or hover. No rule, no reason (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/013-hov.png). That is on my dealbreaker list.
2. The score. It went 100, 97, 95, 94, 93 while approval, stability and legitimacy rose (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/017-m1.png). The only explanation was "war weariness weighs on the public mood". I cannot audit it. The mission wants 115+, so I was falling away from the goal for reasons I could not see.
3. Autopilot. Approve all suggestions, then Execute, kept me alive, brought tension from 74 to about 50, and no crisis punished it. One repeated move wins is my RC gate. I cannot say active play beats it, because the game never showed me what active play would buy.
4. Repetition. The Afghan leader call with "Offer talks on a ceasefire" returned at months 5, 7 and 8 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/034-dm7.png). The assassination crisis reused the refugee rally art (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/025-desk2.png).
5. Safety. Enter in the search box queued a nuclear strike with no confirmation. I removed it, but I only noticed because I looked.
6. Map mode. I switched to Map at month 12 to check parity and every screenshot timed out (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-00/shots/dan-term/map attempts, none saved). The machine was heavily loaded, so I cannot call it a bug, but I could not test map vs desk.

**Capital** sat at 1 of 3 almost always; I never learned the refill rule.

**Would I continue?** Not tonight. I would come back if the score were explained and the staff picks were sometimes wrong.

Scores: fun 5, clarity 6, visual 8, game-feel 7, agency 5, realism 6, replay 5, recommend 5.
