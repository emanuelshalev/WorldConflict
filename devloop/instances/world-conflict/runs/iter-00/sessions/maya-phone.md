# Maya, phone, Flashpoint "Bab al-Mandab Burning" as Saudi Arabia (4 months played)

The title screen won me in a second: globe, "Today's crisis", a 15 MIN tab, one big "Take office" button (shots/maya-phone/001-title.png). The intro tells me the same thing three times, and I skimmed it (002-s1.png).

The desk is the best part. Staff already picked an answer for me, "Approve 4", "Execute month". I never needed a manual (003-s2.png). After I tapped Approve the badge turned into "Your call" and I could not tell whether my pick had been kept (004-s3.png).

Month 1 was a delight: a back channel and the Yemen war was over. The debrief has pictures of a summit, my approval and stability, "-6.5" in red that I did not understand (005-m1.png, 009-m4.png). Month 2 was the hostages. I took the staff pick, special forces. The debrief showed a red X and "this will come back" and nothing about whether it worked. I found out only from the newspaper a month later that every hostage came home. I liked that headline (038-e4.png), but it should have told me right away (018-t2.png).

What annoyed me:
- Houthis and Trump both answered "Good. Words like these travel fast in ... Do not make me regret them." Same headline too, "takes the call, and keeps the door ajar" (025-t3.png). Repeated cards are why I quit games.
- Mission 2/4 never changed and tapping it did nothing. 2/4 of what? What is the goal? This is my "am I winning?" problem again (042-mis2.png). The pill also runs off the edge of the screen.
- Each month is Execute, three debrief pages, a newspaper to scroll, then "To your desk". Tapping the tab chips did not work, only the bottom button.
- "Noted and filed. The machinery of government turns." from my intelligence chief is filler (032-t3e.png).
- Everything was very slow for me, 30 seconds or more to respond at times. The machine was heavily loaded, so I cannot blame the game fully, but I would have closed it.

I would have quit at month 5 (Feb 2027) on the dead Mission pill, with no idea how close I was to the goal. I played 4 of 12 months.

Scores: fun 7, clarity 5, visual 8, game-feel 7, agency 6, realism 6, replay 5, recommend 6.

Biggest wish: a goal line on the desk with real progress that I can tap, and a result card that says in one sentence what my choice did.
