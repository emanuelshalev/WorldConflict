## Verdict: APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

The blocking item from review 3 is fixed, and fixed the right way: while a confirmation is open the coach bubble is hidden (visibility, box kept), so the face only has the bar to clear. In every press I made with the coach on step 3 (no target and Russia, both phones, top, middle, 70% and lowest) Cancel and Give the order were uncovered at the centre and at all four corners, a touch tap on Give the order queued the order every time, and the bubble came back in exactly the same place (same box to the pixel) after Cancel, Escape and Give. The page never scrolled. The smaller items of review 3 are resolved: opened rows run their forecast full width with Add at the top; the hint shows only without a target; search hits tap to the forecast like list rows; the 49 px move on Cancel near the top is gone.

What remains is not blocking. Caveat A is real and bigger at 360x740 (a third of the visible list's height), but the bubble is opaque and visibly lies over the button, it is month 1 only, a scroll clears it and the end of the list now scrolls clear of it; it is a condition. Caveat B: the gallery faces fit easily, but the real nuclear strike face (US seat) is 367 px tall at 360x740 against 342 px of stage, and it is placed so that the first line of its question sits under the desk tabs while 52 px stay empty below the answers. Both answers, including Hold to authorize, are whole and pressable, and the mission warning and consequences are on screen, so it is not misleading or unreachable; it is an S3 to fix in the next round. Two S4 polish items are new (end of list restore off by 71 px; an "Enter" key badge on phone search hits).

**1. Blocking item.** Coach after adding Mobilize: "When you are ready, run the month. Step 3 of 3", at 188,660 190x91 (390x844) and 176,527 172x112 (360x740). Presses, each with Cancel or Escape and then again with Give the order:

- Russia: Mass troops facing Russia and Strike military targets just under the stage top; Strike military targets at 50% and lowest; Arm rebels and Strike nuclear sites at 70% (390); Hard crackdown (the last danger row) at its lowest.
In every face: Cancel and Give the order uncovered at the centre and at the three corners (`c tl tr bl` ok); the fourth corner probe 3 px inside the bottom right returns the button row itself on some faces, which is the button's rounded corner, not a cover (a 6 px inset on the US seat gives `11111` on every face). During the face the bubble is `visibility: hidden; pointer-events: none`; after Cancel, Escape and Give it is visible again at the same coordinates and size. Every touch tap on Give the order queued the order (`order queued`, face closed). Faces end above the bar: 390 answers 525 to 704, bar top 743; 360 answers 525 to 599, bar top 639. Page scroll 0 throughout. Seen: `i1-russia-airstrike-military-lowest-390x844.jpg` shows the face whole and the bubble gone. At 360x740 the 70% position puts Add under the bubble, so there is no face to test there (that is caveat A, item 2).

**2. Caveat A.** With the coach on step 3 the bubble covers the lowest 83 px of the 446 px visible list at 390x844 (19% of its height, 9% of its area) and the lowest 112 px of 342 px at 360x740 (33% of its height, 16% of its area), on the right side where Add sits. A 4 px scan of Add positions from the stage top to the bar: Add's centre is not pressable at 22% of positions at 390 (10 of 67 wholly covered) and 31% at 360 (17 of 74 wholly covered). An Add can be 100% under the bubble (`c2-add-under-bubble-airstrike-military-360x740.jpg`): a tap there does nothing at all (no face, no order, the bubble stays). The new bottom padding works: with the list scrolled to its end the last row's Add (Hard crackdown) is clear of the bubble at both sizes (`c2-list-end-*.jpg`). Judgement: not blocking. The bubble is near opaque and plainly sits on the row, so the player sees why the button does not respond and scrolls; it shows only in month 1 from the first order of the player's own until Execute month; no order is lost or given by mistake. But at 360x740 a third of the list is behind it for a whole month, which is too much for a tutorial hint; tracked as condition CA.
