# maya-desk, iteration 4 (desktop 1920x1080, seed CO-UKR-MAYA1)

I won: Ukraine Flashpoint, Mission complete, grade A, 688/1000, ceasefire signed in month 12. Then I started a second game (Hormuz Convoy) from the end screen. Screenshots are in `shots/maya-desk/`.

## What it was like
The seed box worked, but I only found it after pressing "Play a seed" and the little arrow looks like decoration (002-seed.png). I chose "Guide me" for the news question; crisis texts stayed short, though names like Merz, Takaichi, Burnham, IAEA, Muscat and IRGC stayed foreign to me and I never tapped the underlines.

Each month I guessed the same thing before pressing: "decide the big call, the rest is staff stuff." I was right most months. Pressing the big button without choosing only let the crisis pass with its default, so it is a decision, not a click-through, but the middle months (4 to 8) felt like Choose all, pick, Execute.

## Advice
The Chief of Staff, Foreign, Defence and Interior ministers each recommend things. I mostly followed the Chief. But "Choose all recommended" does not choose the big recommended answer, so I pressed it every month for nothing, and in month 1 the Chief of Staff's advice differed between the centre and the right panel (007-m1c.png). I never understood who recommends what.

## Numbers I did not trust
- Mission line "Keep Washington engaged 39 to 48" vs card "US view of you 37 to 43" (021-m4r.png); also 46 to 60 vs 42 to 57.
- "You start next month with 5" turned into 3 once I clicked more choices; the count moves as you click.
- Header score fell from 100 to 89 while all goals were on track, then the end said 688/1000.

## Map mode (months 4 and 5)
I could do everything because the decision stays in the left panel. The globe has dozens of unlabeled icons I did not press, and it swung to America by itself (019-m4map.png, 022-m5.png). I would stay on the desk.

## Buttons
From month 7 the Next month button was cut off at the bottom (025-m6r.png) and Next month opens the newspaper first, so two presses.

## Repeats
Month 12 brought back "Energy price shock" exactly as in month 2 (044-m12b.png). That was my round-1 deal-breaker, though I did not quit.

## Delight
Result cards with a scene and a street quote (010-m1r.png), the Moments screen telling me why I won (053-mom.png), "The country came round: 56% to 78%" (055-seen.png), achievements with rarity and a ladder (058-rank.png).

## The end
Verdict, achievements, moments, how seen, numbers, truth, ranking: long but each screen was short and I understood the win. The way back had four buttons (Replay same opening, New world, Compare runs, New game); I pressed New game and landed on the title, clear enough.

## Scores
Recommend 7, clarity 6, fun 7. No quit point; I would have been annoyed at the repeat and the cut-off button but kept going.

Biggest wish: one big, always visible "Next" button and a "Choose all" that really takes the staff's answer.
