I played 12 months. I found the seed box (Play a seed, 002-seed.png), entered the code, answered "I know this" and took office.

**Monthly check (my notes before pressing).** Month 1: one vote on Iran sanctions; if I just press Execute the cabinet abstains and the two recommended orders do not run. I was right, and the screen said so in plain words ("If you execute now"). That sentence is the best thing on the desk. Most months, though, were 5 to 8 cards of which one or two were real decisions; the rest I cleared with "Choose all recommended". It felt like a decision in about one month in three.

**What worked for me**
- "Why your score moved" (017-now.png): four parts, each line with a size, 15 percent drift stated. This is what I came for.
- Forecasts on order cards matched results (039-fc2.png, 041-r5.png).
- National address: the report said "Approval +2.4, smaller each time" (048-r6c.png). A repeated move is punished and told so. That answers my dominant-strategy fear for this move.
- Meeting limit: "Your calendar is full" (043-pre6.png). A real constraint.
- Map mode (months 3 and 9 to 12): clicking Ukraine opened all orders against it with chances (056-mapc.png). Everything I needed from the desk was there. The left card covers part of the globe.

**Advice:** I always knew who recommended what (Interior, Finance, Intelligence), and the quote said why. I followed the Interior Minister for mission help and ignored Foreign Minister "Reassure", which appeared nearly every month.

**What hurt**
- Trust: the Finance Minister briefing says Debt-to-GDP 2000% (009-staff.png). Stability fell 54 to 44 in month 9, only 3 explained (060-r9c.png).
- The top-bar capital number is what is left after my plan, not what I have, and the label says "what orders cost" (055-m9.png). Capital also capped at 6 and leaked.
- "Your calls 1 open" never told me which call; the month 3 assassination crisis came back in month 13.
- Month 11 Kim call was month 3 again, word for word; one quote is reused for different choices (063-m11.png, 027-r3.png). That is fake depth.
- Edge test: I struck Turkey's nuclear facilities in month 12 and got approval +4, stability +4, Erdogan "keeps the door ajar" (067-r12b.png). A free win. That is exactly my deal breaker.
- A suggested order (defense pact to China) was disabled every month.
- Typing in the orders search with no focus fired hotkeys and flipped the mode (029-cf.png).
- Unknown names: Mojtaba Khamenei, Andy Burnham, Sanae Takaichi, Kramatorsk; "the network" in month 10 never named. The explanation setting did not help because I chose "I know this".

**Not done:** no end screen in 12 of 48 months, so no second game and I cannot judge the finale. I could not tell if score 115 is reachable (score drifted 100 to 90).

**Scores:** fun 7, clarity 7, visual 8, game feel 7, agency 7, realism 5, replay 6, recommend 6.
