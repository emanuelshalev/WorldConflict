# Ruth (ruth-desk), iteration 4, seed CO-UKR-RUTH1, desktop 1920x1080

Screenshots: `devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-04/shots/ruth-desk/` (numbered).

I played the whole Flashpoint, 12 months, as Ukraine and reached the end. Mission complete, grade B, 634. I chose "Some help" at the news question: the underlined names were fine, but I mostly did not need them.

**Each month, my guess versus what happened.** Month 1 I thought: one answer to the economic-zone question and press the big button. Right, but the big button would have left the crisis open, and I was not sure what that meant (004, 005). Months 2 to 12 followed one pattern: pick an answer to the crisis, pick the mission move, press "Choose all recommended", Execute. By month 8 it felt like clicking through, except when the crisis was a real argument. Month 5 (guarantee on paper) and month 6 (land for a promise) were the best: I picked a ratified treaty and a referendum as a history teacher would (026, 030).

**Advice.** I understood who recommends what (Foreign Minister, Defense Minister, Chief of Staff) and why, because each card says it. I followed them about half the time. When two ministers disagreed I liked that.

**Map mode (month 3).** Same decision panel on the left, so I could do everything. But the map is covered in round icons all named "Map marker", and I could not tell what they meant without the Guide. I would not choose the map to decide (017).

**What annoyed me.**
- Month 2: Choose did nothing when I had no political capital, and said nothing. Later cards said "calendar is full", so it can be done. Silent buttons are my deal-breaker (014).
- "If you leave it: Emergency security powers" (010): leaving a crisis open picks an answer I did not choose. Month 1 said it passes with no effect.
- Two Washington numbers on one card: 36 to 42 and 34 to 40 (025). Verdict says final score 86 against par 102 though my goal was 82 (055).
- Cards clipped at the bottom (005); Next month half cut off (029).
- Names I did not know: Takaichi, Carney. Patriots, IAEA and Enerhodar I could guess. "Arsenal" for a security guarantee was odd.

**What I loved.** The truth screen (060): my world next to the real one, with sources, "Better" and "Worse". That is exactly what I came for. The Chronicle newspaper and the callback "Hostages released after secret talks" were delightful. I want to read more behind each real event; the sources are small links.

**The end.** The count, verdict, achievements, moments, how you were seen, numbers, truth, ranking: long but never confusing, with Back and Next always visible. Month 12's own result card was skipped (054). The ranking page (061) is full of gamer talk: "Competent, top 10%", "Random staff", "ladder". On the truth screen "1955k" meant nothing to me.

**Way back.** After the ranking I saw "Replay same opening", "New world", "Compare runs" (greyed) and "New game". I pressed "New game", it took me to the title screen, and "Take office as Ukraine" started a second game at once. Clear enough, though I was not sure what "New world" did.

**Scores.** Recommend 7. I would not have quit. Wish: tell me, in plain words and always, why a button will not work.
