# Priya, desktop 1920x1080, CO-UKR-PRIY1, Ukraine Flashpoint, 12 months

Screenshots: `devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-04/sessions/priya-desk/`.

**Result.** Played all 12 months, reached the reveal, pressed New game and started a Taiwan Flashpoint. No quit point. Verdict: Mission complete, A, 696.

**Setup.** The seed box was easy to find (title screen, "Play a seed"). I answered "I know this" to the news question. Clean text, as wanted.

**Each month (my guess before, result after).** Month 1: I thought "one crisis call, then Execute". Right, and the big button alone would have left the call open with no effect. Most months I was right: one crisis decision, a mission move, a pile of meetings I could skip. Months 4 (Blackout winter), 7 (manpower wall) and 12 (the signing table) felt like real decisions. Months 2, 5, 6 and 10 felt like clicking through, because "Choose all recommended" took care of everything else.

**Advice.** I understood who recommends what. Foreign Minister pushes the coalition and Washington, Defense Minister the front, Interior Minister the home front. Followed them about half the time. I overrode them on Iran sanctions (voted yes, they said abstain) and on month 12 (put it to the people, as the Ukrainian constitution would require).

**Numbers (trust).** Several did not match:
- Month 4: mission line "Washington 41 to 45", detail "38 to 44".
- Month 6: "UK relations 88 to 94", detail "86 to 90".
- Month 8: UN vote YES 11 NO 2 on the card, YES 8 NO 5 in the paper.
- Month 9: paper says approval 67 to 74, header says 61 to 73.
- Header capital reads "3 of 6" for what is left.
- "Choose all recommended" spent all my capital on a 2-point Reform program without asking.

**Map mode (months 5 and 6).** It opened on the United States, not Ukraine. "Whole world" did nothing, zoom out made a tiny ball, and the Map Guide covered the Choose button. The cards in Map mode say only "Mixed: hurts 1 goal, helps 1", not which goal. I went back to Desk to decide. `sessions/priya-desk/024-map5c.png`, `027-map5f.png`.

**Clickable or not.** The "Your country" dropdown stayed open for the rest of the game over buttons. The "I know this" top button looks like a label. The REFUSED row in the newspaper does nothing when tapped. Reveal sidebar items jump only sometimes. The Next month button was cut off on tall result cards.

**Realism.** Real: the referendum, draft age 25 to 23, Enerhodar, London and Paris reassurance force, Moscow calling troops legitimate targets. Doubtful: Andy Burnham appears as UK prime minister with no explanation (Moreau in France is explained, which makes this worse), the ticker says "Saudi Arabia smashes through defenses" every month, and "Russia gained barely 100 km2 net in 2026, by ISW's count" in the Truth screen looks wrong to me. Names I did not know: none of the geopolitics. Unclear: "Defense budget 0% used", "Meetings you take 0 of 3" next to "4 meetings".

**End.** The sequence is long but good. The Truth screen, with sources and BETTER/WORSE tags, is why I would recommend it. But the verdict stamps "MISSION COMPLETE: A peace that holds" while the seal "A Peace That Holds" is locked and the Truth says no peace. Pick one. The ranking says both "first run: ladder of 108" and "better than 100%". The way back was clear: Replay same opening, New world, New game. New game dropped me on the title screen, which still remembered my record. Taiwan was on the far side of the globe; I found it with the arrow by the dropdown.

**Biggest wish.** One true number everywhere, and a verdict that agrees with the debrief.
