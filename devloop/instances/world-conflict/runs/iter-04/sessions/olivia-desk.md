**Quit point:** the month 2 desk, about 3 minutes in. Seven things to open, six recommended orders, four columns of text (`008-m2.png`).

## What happened
The title globe is gorgeous (`001-title.png`). "Play a seed" looked greyed out at first, but it opened a small box, I typed the code and the game started as the US president at once. Good. The "How well do you follow world news" question was easy: I chose Guide me. The mission page behind it opened half scrolled and read like a form (`003-afterseed.png`).

**Month 1.** I expected: pick one answer, press the big button. Mostly right, but the desk is four columns of small text (`004-m1.png`). The three Choose buttons look the same, and I ended up with the answer marked "Hurts", not the one marked best (`005-m1b.png`). Then I pressed Choose all recommended and Execute. Result: a handshake picture and a huge **+10** (`007-m1result.png`). That was the best moment of my session. But the bar says 20 to 30 and the mission line says 20 to 29.

**Month 2.** I expected one choice. I got "7 things" (`008-m2.png`). I just pressed Choose all recommended again, which worked, but I did not decide anything. The side box said one order would not run, then it did.

**Month 3, Map mode.** I could still decide in the left panel, so nothing was missing, but the globe is covered with about 40 little icons I cannot read (`012-m3map.png`).

**Months 4 and 5.** Pressing Desk gave me a whole newspaper page I did not ask for (`016-m4.png`). The month 5 result talks about a call I never opened (`019-m4result.png`).
