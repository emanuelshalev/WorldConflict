# Sam, desktop 1920x1080, control SL-CHN-SAM01 (4 months)

**Verdict:** more crafted than I expected, not yet a game I would recommend without caveats. Recommend 5/10.

**Seed code:** found "Play a seed" under the title copy, typed the code, started China. Clear (sessions/sam-desktop/002-seed.png). I kept "Some help"; the Who is who strip and "i names" chips explained Scarborough Shoal and Sara Duterte in a line and stayed out of the way (003-afterseed.png).

**Delight:** the title globe with the serif "Pick a fire" headline and live ticker (001-title.png); illustrated result scenes with a DONE stamp (009-r1.png, 015-r2.png, 017-r3.png). Frostpunk-level mood.

**Monthly guesses.** M1: decide Scarborough, big button executes what I picked. Right on the crisis, wrong that the two recommended orders also needed my eye (I hit "Choose all recommended" by accident, 006-x.png; same label as the card buttons). M2: seven things, I guessed the big button applies the cabinet's picks. Right ("Skip for now: your cabinet decides"), but month 1's Skip meant "crisis passes with no effect". M3: choose-all then Execute, and that is when I felt I was clicking through (016-m3.png).

**Advice:** Foreign Minister, Finance Minister, Chief of Staff and Interior Minister all recommend. I understood who and the one-line why, and followed them since they never conflicted. That is why decisions did not feel like decisions.

**Craft defects**
- Recommendation text overflows the third answer card (004-m1.png), a flaw Suzerain's fixed frames would never allow.
- Five counters for one resource: capital "3 of 6", Orders, Your calls, "3 things", "3 now".
- Same Skip button, opposite results. "Your call" names a crisis, a mission move and a counter; mission, goal and goals-on-track are mixed.
- Condemn publicly and Reinforce and shadow show identical effect text (010-m2.png).

**Numbers (trust)**
- M1: Approval stamp -6, details say 64 to 62 (-2); relation -56 vs -57 (009-r1.png).
- M3 header capital 4 of 6, side panel 5 now (016-m3.png). M4 header 6 of 6, card 5 to 4.
- M4 stability: card -3, tile -2 (017-r3.png).

**Map mode (M2).** Found markers, a country order sheet and Execute. But the same order says "Add to this month" with coin icons here and "Choose" with a diamond cost on the desk; markers are all unlabelled "Map marker"; "free", "6% defense" and "95% chance" are unexplained (013-map2.png, 014-map3.png). The ORDERED stamp is lovely. After adding, the left panel jumped back to card 1. Parity: fail on wording, pass on outcome.

**Quit point:** month 3 desk, as above. I stopped at month 4, did not reach the end, and did not start a second game.

**Biggest wish:** one capital number that every screen agrees with.
