# marcus-term, iteration 4 (build 2083597), desktop 1920x1080

I played the control game (seed T1990-USA-MARC1, One Term, Bush, Jan 1990) for 12 months, months 5 and 6 on the Map. I chose "I know this" on the familiarity question. I did not play a second game and did not reach the election.

## What I thought each month
Months 2 to 12 I expected "a few calls, one crisis, press the big button". For months 2 to 4 I was right and the result card explained my own choices. From month 5 the calls repeated (Thatcher, Mulroney, Kaifu: Promise full support) so "Choose all recommended" became the game. Month 10 I expected another call and got an Outbreak crisis. Month 12 had eight items, the most of any.

## Good
- Result cards tell me what my choice did, with numbers and a quote (`sessions/marcus-term/007-r1.png`). That is what I came for.
- Check what will happen before Execute; the capital arithmetic added up (`010-m2c.png`).
- Map mode: tap Iran, get orders with odds and the reason an order is unavailable (`025-map2.png`). I found everything I needed on the Map in months 5 and 6, but the open order menu covers the Execute button.
- Delayed consequences ("outcome in 3 months", "Remember Soviet Union's offer?").

## Not good
- **Mission wording (naming).** Month 1 said "Mission: Iran needs -45+, now -75. No goal moves that we can see." The mission is to win an election. I only learned in month 2 that the second goal is to mend ties with Iran. The Iran number then showed -82 to -75 beside "Iran's view of you -74 to -69" (`029-r5b.png`). Which counts?
- **Unexplained swings (trust).** Tension +13 in month 6 (only a ticker line about a Pakistani test; the Why? link jumped to another page, `030-why1.png`). Approval 74 to 67 and stability 72 to 62 in month 10 with a French phone call as the lead (`048-r9.png`); the flu outbreak that caused it only appeared as the next crisis.
- **1990 is not 1990.** The Soviet Union carries the Russian tricolor (`042-r7.png`). A US general strike in February. Japan bombs North Korea in July, then South Korea. Japan asks for a naval base in the US. "Back the stabilization force" in Gaza. US debt 123% of GDP. Iraq sanctions in June with "constructive signal" from Saddam, and August passes quietly. As a what-if I can forgive some; as a check on plausibility, no.
- **Repetition.** The month 1 incident came back in month 11 with the same three answers, a different country.
- **Art.** A phone call shows "377 seats majority won" (`045-r8.png`). Decorative, but it reads like a result.
- **Affordance.** "Add to this month" did nothing when pressed by label in the Map order menu, the same card worked by position (`027-r5.png`). "All orders" on the desk did nothing on the first press. Court Iran took six steps on the desk, three on the Map.
- **Replay.** I never found "replay this month differently" in the Menu (`058-menu.png`: Cabinet, World, Chronicle, Codex, Settings, Save and load).

## Advice
I understood who said what (Interior, Finance, Foreign Minister, Chief of Staff, Intelligence Chief). The "Helps: Mend ties with Iran (bonus)" tag was the most useful. I followed the Foreign Minister on calls and the Interior Minister on the flu (lockdown, because the card said best for mission). Staff disagreeing felt like a real decision only twice (strike, outbreak).

## Knowledge flags
2026 title screen: "Ceasefire or Collapse", "Bab al-Mandab", "Hormuz Convoy" meant nothing to me; the "i" and "What is going on here?" were there but I did not open them. In the 1990 game I knew every name.
