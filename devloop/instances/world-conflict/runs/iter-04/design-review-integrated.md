  - Capabilities on a month 2 save (Hormuz, United States, seed 4242): Your own orders, Everything view, Mission panel, Explain, Your country, Menu, See all in Map mode (`extras.mjs`, `log-extras.txt`, screenshots `x-*`).
  - A Term game from the title (One Term, 2026, default seat United States), months 1 and 2 (`play.mjs 2 t term`, `log-play-t.txt`, screenshots `t-*`).
  - The reveal at the end of a Flashpoint via `devloop/kit/finish-flashpoint.mjs` at 1920x1080 (Hormuz, United States, 12 months fast-forwarded; screenshots `r-reveal-*.png` copied from that run).
- Every screenshot cited below was looked at.
- Not counted: the UI dev server runs React StrictMode, so "Ask your staff" posted its opening question twice (`p-04-askstaff.jpg`); check it in a production build.

## Verdict: NOT APPROVED

The integrated desk is close to the screen the designs promised at 1920x1080: one state band, the month list, the thing and the plan side by side, no page scroll and no inner scroll on the month (layout facts in every log: `pageScroll 1080/1080`, no scrollers, no clipped regions), the All orders catalogue with family tabs that fit, and an in-place danger confirmation. What stops publishing is what the screen says, not where it sits: the plan, the hold card and the result card state political capital numbers that are never true, adviser quotes contradict the card they sit on, the mission tag counts helps as hurts, and the two order-choice screens a player meets every month (Recommended orders, The move for your mission) are laid out as narrow word-per-line columns with badges over the text. Map mode still shows North America for crises in Iran and Gaza. Seven S2 findings, no S1.

## Capabilities of the old desk (judging point 5)

All reachable at 1920x1080: orders by family (All orders, six family tabs with counts, all tiles in view, `p-32-military.jpg`), search (field in All orders), targets (picker with likely targets and relation tags, "Pick on the map", `p-30-target-open.jpg`), staff suggestions (Recommended orders, Your own orders), advisers (Ask your staff, DR-INT-6), cabinet, world, chronicle, codex, settings, save and load (Menu and the Mission panel tabs, `x-05-mission.jpg`, `x-08-menu.jpg`), explain (Explain and "How much?"), map parity (the plan, Execute and See all are in Map mode, `x-09-map-seeall.jpg`). Nothing found missing.

## Three things that work well

1. All orders: family tabs with counts, every tile in view, a target picker that leads with the countries in the crisis, and unavailable orders that say why and offer the right one ("You are at war with Iran. Propose a ceasefire instead").
2. The danger confirmation: it takes the panel in place, lists what the staff expect in plain words, and the safe button (Cancel) has the focus.
3. The state band and month list: one line of truth that updates as you choose (orders, meetings, defense budget, calls open), "Decided: ..." on each list row, and no page or inner scroll anywhere on the month at 1920x1080.
