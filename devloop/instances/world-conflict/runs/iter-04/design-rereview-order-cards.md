# Design re-review: build-order-cards (D-81)

Reviewer: UX Designer and Art Director. Branch `build-order-cards` at 3fa1fa5 (full verify passed), after the review fixes 51409c0 (DR-OC-1 to 3), their guards f5103c7, baselines 0a01603 and the follow-up 3fa1fa5 (the confirmation scrolls only its nearest scrolling box). First review: `design-review-order-cards.md` at 6c18b63 (CHANGES REQUIRED).
Seat: Ceasefire or Collapse, Ukraine, seed code CO-UKR-MAYA1, pinned through `_lib.mjs` `startGame` / `pinSeat`, served by the running dev servers (UI 5173, API 8080).
Sizes: 3440x1440 (CEO, first), 1920x1080, 1440x900, 1366x768 (one desktop session resized), 1024x768 (tablet session), 390x844 (phone session). Gallery at 1440x900 and 390x844.
Method: Playwright kit sessions driven by `design-rereview-order-cards/_scripts/recapture.mjs` (built on the first review's `capture.mjs`), plus three probes: `second-click.mjs` (what a second press does after the map menu reflows), `face-reach.mjs` (a danger order pressed wherever it sits on screen: are both answers visible and on top), `gallery2.mjs` / `gal-spy.mjs` (stamp, "free", tile heights). `devloop/kit/layout-audit.mjs` with the `game` config on the catalogue (no target, Russia), month 2 Desk, the month 2 Map menu and the Map menu with the face open. Geometry facts in `facts-*.txt`, audit tables in `audit-*.txt`, probe output in `probe-*.txt`. Screenshots read side by side with the first review's.

## Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED

The three required changes are done well where they were asked for: the Map menu face is whole, on one line, title once, at every size; the catalogue speaks one vocabulary; the phone has rows. But the confirmation's new "scroll into view" does not know about the phone's fixed bottom bar, and the phone rows make the face grow about 220 px downward from the row. Result: on the phone desk catalogue, a danger order pressed in the lower half of the list opens a face whose Cancel and Give the order sit entirely under the bottom bar, with **Execute month** exactly where Give the order should be. That is the same defect class that blocked the first review (the player must scroll to find the answers), on the most cramped screen, next to the month's most consequential button. It is a small fix; everything else can follow as conditions.

## New problems

1. **S2, phone desk catalogue: the danger face's answers open under the fixed bottom bar, with Execute month where Give the order should be.** `ui/src/components/orders/ConfirmFace.tsx` (the `requestAnimationFrame` scroll block added in 51409c0 / 3fa1fa5) against the phone desk's fixed bar (`aside.cmd-bar`, `div.bar-row` with `button.wc-btn.primary` "Execute month"). The nearest scrolling box (`#desk-stage`) runs under the bar, so the code finds the buttons "inside" its box and does not scroll. Probe (`probe-face-reach-phone.txt`): Strike military targets pressed with its row top at y=379: buttons at 616-664, fine. Pressed at y=590 or y=717, and Hard crackdown at any position (it is the last row): Cancel and Give at 748-796, entirely under the bar; the element on top of Give's centre is the Execute month button. Seen in `reach-airstrike-military-390x844-70pct.jpg` and `desk-confirm-390x844.jpg`. Desktop is fine at 1366 and 1440 for the same presses (`probe-face-reach-desktop.txt`). Before this branch's fixes the tile was about as tall as the face, so the face rarely grew past its own tile; the phone rows make it grow about 220 px down.
   Fix: give the stage a scroll padding equal to the bar on the phone (`#desk-stage { scroll-padding-bottom: calc(<bar height> + 8px) }`, or a CSS variable the bar already sets), and in `ConfirmFace` take the box's bottom as `c.bottom - parseFloat(getComputedStyle(box).scrollPaddingBottom)` (and top likewise), or call `btns.scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })` scoped to the box, which honours scroll padding. Extend DR-OC-1 (or add DR-OC-4): phone 390x844, desk All orders, target Russia, put Strike military targets at 70% of the viewport height, press it, assert `document.elementFromPoint` at the centre of Cancel and of Give the order is that button. Also run it on the last row (Hard crackdown).
2. **S3, Map menu: when the face opens, the menu header scrolls away and stays away after Cancel.** `ui/src/styles/orders.css` `.map-action-menu` / `.mam-head`, and the scroll block in `ConfirmFace.tsx`. To bring the buttons into view the menu scrolls by 45 px (1440), 227 px (1366) and 287 px (1024): the header (Russia, relation chip, capital left, close) leaves the menu's visible box, and at 1024 the Propose ceasefire / Mass troops tiles above the face are cut at the top. After Cancel the menu stays scrolled (140 px at 1366, 170 px at 1024, 45 px at 1440 after Escape), so the player is back in a menu with no country name and no close button in view (`map-after-cancel-1024x768.jpg`, `facts-*.txt` "after Cancel"). Fix: make `.mam-head` sticky inside the menu (`position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 1; background: <menu bg>`), so it never scrolls away; and restore the menu's previous `scrollTop` when the face closes (store it when the face opens).
3. **S3, phone catalogue rows hide the forecast and the blurb, and a tap on an available row queues it.** `ui/src/styles/orders.css` `.ord-cat-rows .ord-meta-blurb { display: none }`, and the row variant of `OrderCard.tsx`, which has no `ord-info` (How much? Forecast) button; `activate` on an available row calls `give()`. On the phone the player can no longer read "Call up the reserves. Rivals read it as a threat." or the relations and tension forecast of a non-danger order (Spy, Sabotage, Denounce, Mobilize) before adding it. The CSS comment says the blurb "waits for the forecast (a tap)", but no tap opens a forecast for an available row. Queued orders can be removed for free, so this is a loss of information, not a trap. Fix: let the row's press open the forecast (`setInfo`) and keep Add as the only way to queue from a phone catalogue row, or add a small "Forecast" link on the meta line with a 44 px hit area. Seen on `catalog-russia-at-mil-390x844.jpg` / `catalog-russia-at-intel-390x844.jpg`, behaviour read in code.
4. **S3, Map menu: the reflow puts a different order under the pointer, and its hover forecast opens next to the danger face.** `.ord-mam-grid { grid-auto-flow: row dense }` plus `.ord-menu .ord-wrap.is-confirming { grid-column: 1 / -1 }`. Strike military targets sits in the right column of row 1; when its face opens it moves to a full-width row 2 and Mass troops facing Russia slides under the pointer, so its hover preview ("Mass troops facing Russia: Relations -8 now, -3/month, Tension +0.5/month") opens beside the Strike confirmation, over Propose ceasefire. A player reading numbers next to a risky question can take them for the strike's. Seen at 3440 (`map-confirm-3440x1440-CEO.jpg`) and 1440 (`map-reflow-under-pointer-airstrike-military-1440x900.jpg`). Fix: suppress `HoverPreview` for every card in a container that has an open `.ord-confirm` (or while any face is open), and consider opening the face in place as an overlay over the grid (absolute, full menu width, top at the pressed tile's top) so nothing reflows.
5. **S4, catalogue at wide sizes is taller than before.** Six family groups instead of four categories: at 5 tiles a row this is 9 rows instead of 7, with Economy holding one tile (Reform) and Nuclear two. Hidden content in the catalogue scroller at 3440 grows from 2,504 to 2,895 px (no target) and 2,888 to 3,314 px (Russia), at 1920 from 3,247 to 3,638 and 3,631 to 4,057 px. It falls at 1440, 1366 and 1024 (tiles are shorter). A preference: at 1920 and up, let small groups share a row (for example Nuclear beside Military's last row, Economy beside Home), each keeping its heading.
