## Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED

One item, small. The S2 is fixed against the bar: with no coach on screen, both answers now end above the command bar at every position, on both phones, with the page never scrolling and the list returning to where it was. But the bar is not the only thing that lies over the stage's bottom. In month 1, once the player adds any order of their own (and many first-time players do exactly that in the catalogue), the coach bubble "When you are ready, run the month. Step 3 of 3" sits above Execute month, 91 px tall, over the right part of the stage. The face now lands exactly there: Give the order is 69% covered at 390x844 and almost wholly hidden at 360x740, and a finger on its centre lands on the bubble, which counts as a press outside the face and **cancels it**. The player presses "Give the order", the question vanishes and nothing is queued. That is the same defect class as the S2 (an answer under fixed chrome on the phone) and it is misleading, so it blocks. C1, C3 are resolved; C2 is resolved in behaviour with a layout weakness to follow; C4 I accept as a tracked condition. Nothing else regressed.

## New problems

1. **S2, phone desk catalogue: the month 1 coach bubble covers Give the order, and a tap on it cancels the face.** `ui/src/components/desk/CommandRail.tsx` (`CommandBar`: `<CoachBubble step="execute" place="above" />` inside `.ex-wrap`, outside the box measured for `--cmd-bar-h`), `ui/src/styles/month.css` `.coach-bubble { pointer-events: auto; z-index: 6 }`, and `ConfirmFace.tsx` `onDown` (a pointerdown outside the face cancels it). Reproduced: fresh profile, Full desk, All orders, target Russia, Add on Mobilize (the coach moves to step 3, bubble at 188,660 190x91 at 390x844; 176,527 to 639 at 360x740), then Add on Strike military targets or Hard crackdown in the lower half of the list. Give the order at 173,647 184x48 is 69% covered; `elementFromPoint` at its centre is the coach bubble; a touch tap there closes the face and the order stays available (`probe-coach-give-390x844-*.txt`, `s2-coach-390x844-airstrike-military.jpg`, `s2-coach-after-tap-*.jpg`). At 360x740 only a sliver of the red button shows (`s2-full-russia-policing-hard-bottom-360x740.jpg`). The coach stays on step 3 until the month is executed, so every danger order given from the lower part of the list in a first month meets it. DR-OC-4 does not catch it because it never adds an order of the player's own first. Pre-existing, made visible by the fix: the same bubble also lies over the Add buttons of rows in the lowest 90 px of the list (112 px at 360), a tap there hits the bubble (`probe-phone-clean-360x740.txt`, "no face").
   Fix: do not show the coach bubble while a confirmation is open (one question on screen at a time), for example `CoachBubble` returns null when `document.querySelector('.ord-confirm')` is open (via a store flag set by `ConfirmFace`), or `.desk-screen:has(.ord-confirm) .cmd-bar .coach-bubble { display: none }`; and let `--cmd-bar-h` measure from the top of the bubble when it shows (observe `.ex-wrap .coach-bubble` too), so rows and faces end above it. Extend DR-OC-4: add Mobilize first, check the coach is on step 3, then press Add on Strike military targets at 70% and Hard crackdown at its lowest, at 390x844 and 360x740, and assert `elementFromPoint` at the centre of both answers is the button, and that a tap on Give queues the order.
2. **S3, phone catalogue: an opened row is cramped and its Add button floats.** `ui/src/styles/orders.css` `.ord-cat-rows .ord-row > .wc-card .wc-card-body { padding-right: 92px }`, `.ord-cat-rows .ord-act { top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%) }`, and the art column centring. When the forecast opens, the description and forecast are set in a column about 180 px wide (word-per-line wrapping: "about / +20"), the row grows from 74 to 379 to 421 px, and both the Add button and the order's plate move to the vertical middle of that tall row, away from the title (`c2-tap-mobilize-screen-390x844.jpg`). It works, but it looks broken and pushes the next rows a full screen down. Fix: while the row is open (`.ord-wrap:has(.ord-expand)` or the `data-row-forecast` attribute), pin Add and the plate to the top (`top: 12px; transform: none`), and let `.ord-expand` run the card's full width below the title line (padding-right 0, or place it in a row spanning art and body). Not blocking.
3. **S4, the new hint shows with a target too.** `OrderCatalog.tsx` `ord-cat-hint`: "Tap an order for its forecast; Add puts it in this month." now always shows in rows mode, 18 px at 390 and 36 px at 360 above the first group, which works against C4. Consider showing it until the player has opened one forecast, or folding it into the first family heading line.
4. **S4, two tap behaviours for the same row.** Read in code (`OrderCard.tsx` comment on `pressForecast`): search hits on the phone keep tap-to-add while the list rows tap to the forecast. Not exercised on screen. Prefer one rule (tap shows, Add adds) in both.
5. **S4, 360x740: Cancel on a face that did not need to move the list moved it by 49 px** (1748 to 1699, `probe-phone-clean-360x740.txt`, first line). Probably the focus returning to the row's Add button near the top edge. Minor; check that focus returns with `preventScroll`.
