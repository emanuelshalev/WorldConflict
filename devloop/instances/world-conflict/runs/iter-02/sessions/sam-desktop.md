# Sam, desktop (build 271e377)

I came to decide in five minutes whether this is crafted or AI-made. First verdict: crafted. The title globe with live crisis pins and the serif headline is real art direction (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/001-title.png). The debrief modal, with the stamped +5 and illustrated scene, made me smile (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/006-exec1.png).

Then the cracks.

**Craft defects**
- Month 1 desk: the three response cards are not aligned (card 2 sits higher), the Cabinet pick tag overlaps card 3 and stays there after I picked card 1, and the YOUR PICK stamp hides the free pill (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/005-pick.png). Frostpunk never lets a stamp eat a label.
- STEP ACHIEVED stamp covers Iran's view of you (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/028-fin.png).
- See everything page: China moves against you, Russia moves against you, ORDER National address are empty title cards; half the screen is blank (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/007-every.png). That is the dashboard-of-cards look I dread.
- Desk scrolls itself so the crisis scene is cropped every month (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/008-m2.png).

**Vocabulary**
Political capital in the HUD, "1 of 4 left" and "seals" in the rail, a diamond on cards, "Needs diamond 2" in the map menu. One concept, four names. "Hurts: approval 56%+" reads like a typo. Plague Inc. says one thing once.

**Desk vs map (S2)**
Same order, two different cards: "Add to this month" on the map, "Added" on the desk, different cost displays, and the open crisis is invisible in Map mode (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/010-venez.png, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/009-map.png). The Desk button did not respond once with a menu open (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/012-desk2b.png). This is where I would have quit.

**Knowledge**
IRGC, Isfahan, Bandar Abbas were explained by the i chips and the Who is who strip, which stayed out of the way. Muscat and Fujairah were not. The familiarity prompt was well made; I kept Some help and never needed more.

**End**
I let the cabinet picks carry me, and by month 5 I was 5 of 5 on track. Agency is thin. The end sequence is nicely staged (count-up, stamp, moments timeline devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/032-end4.png), but it said MISSION FAILED, grade D, 0 stars on 682/1000 and 5 of 6 goals, and the last month tile is black "the fall" though nothing fell (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/sam-desktop/034-end6.png). Month 2's Hostages in Afghanistan felt random in an Iran war.

**Scores:** recommend 6, visual 7, feels like a game 7, clarity 6, agency 5.

Biggest wish: one order card, one currency name, and no stamp ever covering text.
