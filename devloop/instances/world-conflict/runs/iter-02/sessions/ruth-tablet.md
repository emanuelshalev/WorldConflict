I picked One Term and the Ceasefire or Collapse card because I read about the Ukraine talks this morning. The globe and era choice were clear and showed 2026 (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/002-term.png`). On the card the big button said "Take office as United States"; Lead Russia and Lead Ukraine were hidden below, so I scrolled to find them (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/004-ukr2.png`). I stayed with the United States.

The familiarity question was a good idea. I took "Some help". The "What is going on here?" briefing, with who is who and a "what really happened" timeline, is exactly what I wanted (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/008-brief.png`).

But my first month was a sanctions vote on Iran, then hostages in Afghanistan. Nothing about Ukraine. I did not understand why a vote to abstain gave "Your forces gain ground against Iran, +5 Front" (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/010-exec1.png`). The mission bar read "-100 to -100, needs -70", which meant nothing to me. The staff also added "Spy on Iran" and the paper said the president ordered it; I never chose that.

I found All orders on my own in month 3, typed Ukraine and tapped "Mediate peace for Ukraine, 15% chance". I liked that locked orders say why in plain words ("You are not at war with Ukraine"). The result said only "Ukraine answered within the month: court United States" (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/028-e3b.png`). Did the talks move? Did Russia answer? I could not tell.

After that I mostly pressed "Approve staff picks" and "Execute month". The paper front page is lovely (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/012-paper.png`), and the Iran war ending with a "Step achieved" stamp was a pleasure (`devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/ruth-tablet/033-c7.png`), though it felt as if I had done very little. A general strike and a stock market crash took the desk in later months.

Words I did not know or the game did not explain: counter-intelligence, foreign interference, "Front", political capital, "TGT LOCK", "CLASSIFIED" labels, and the newer leaders Burnham, Merz, Lee Jae-myung in the call tabs.
