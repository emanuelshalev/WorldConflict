# Maya (phone), Hormuz Convoy as the US, build 5ef3ead

I played today's crisis to the end: 12 months, about 22 minutes, A, 3 stars, 688. I never wanted to quit. Hardly any single month took longer than 30 seconds.

**What worked.** The title globe with "Today's crisis" got me going in one tap (shots/maya-phone/001-title.png). The news-familiarity card was easy; I chose Guide me. The briefing "Who is who" explained Khamenei and the IRGC (shots/maya-phone/003-m1.png). Each month has one clear card, a cabinet pick and Execute. The Mission panel with "Helps now" chips and the "Best for your mission" tag finally answered my old question, what do I do to win (shots/maya-phone/011-goal2.png, shots/maya-phone/013-more.png). The end sequence is great: the Wordle-style month squares (shots/maya-phone/040-m12x.png), "Mission complete", the ranking "better than 81%" (shots/maya-phone/062-e8.png), the share card (shots/maya-phone/066-share.png). I know why I won.

**What tripped me.**
- Names I did not know: Muscat (never explained), Isfahan, bunker-buster, Qatari text, logistics access. The underlines and i help; Muscat had none.
- The top goal chip text is cut off and tapping it did nothing; only the title opens Mission (shots/maya-phone/010-goal.png).
- Picking an option: first tap failed, and I only saw "Your pick" after scrolling (shots/maya-phone/014-pick.png).
- The monthly "what your choice did" said "Your forces gain ground against Iran" two months running, not about my North Korea choice (shots/maya-phone/018-m4d.png). That is the repeated-card thing I hate.
- Newspaper overlay once swallowed my Execute (shots/maya-phone/026-m8d.png).
- The More button is clipped when I have 4 capital (shots/maya-phone/017-m4.png).
- Ending: "Spy on Iran" is my best moment, but I never picked it; Approve cabinet pick bundled it (shots/maya-phone/044-e4.png). Score breakdown (Leadership x1.5, par 107) is spreadsheet talk (shots/maya-phone/042-e2b.png). Title says Master Diplomat then Statesman. Achievements say "rarity not measured yet".

**Scores:** fun 7, clarity 8, visual 8, game feel 7, agency 7, realism 7, replay 7, recommend 8.

Quit point: none.
