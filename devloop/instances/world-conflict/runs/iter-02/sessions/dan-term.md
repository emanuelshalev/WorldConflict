"How much?" turns vague tags into numbers like -2 and +2 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/dan-term/011-how.png). All orders has forecasts and a defence budget share (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/dan-term/028-bw.png). This is what I came for.
