# Priya, priya-flash (laptop), Ceasefire or Collapse as Ukraine, full 12 months

I picked the Ukraine flashpoint from the title globe and opened "What is going on here?" first. Who is who, why it matters and a real timeline up to 11 Nov 2026 (shots/priya-flash/003-about.png). That is exactly what I wanted. The familiarity question took one tap ("I know this") and then stayed out of my way.

## Play
I played 12 months, mostly taking the cabinet pick, but read each crisis. The crises felt like real debates: the minerals-style deal, the Enerhodar plant, lowering the draft age to 23, the coalition of the willing, the signing table at month 12. Using "How much?" and the Forecast chance, I knew what each move was meant to do. When I tried "Mediate peace for Russia" the game told me why not and offered "Propose ceasefire instead" with a 12% chance (018-pc3.png). That is the opposite of an opaque refusal. The ceasefire failed in month 2, and Russia's reply was shown.

## What annoyed me
- Staff pre-add orders that lock my political capital. In month 2 I had two seals and both were already spent (013-m2.png) until I removed the staff order.
- Month 1: card 3 "Reject it and strike" was tagged "Best trade-off" while the cabinet pick was flagged as against my mission (006-m1.png). Which one is right?
- A -9 on US view was stamped "A STEP FORWARD" in green (011-ex1.png).
- Advisor quote layout breaks from month 7: one word per line, with the Cabinet pick button on top of it (040-m11.png).
- Staff kept suggesting "Court Belarus" for Ukraine (026-m5.png). No.
- The same Trump quote about a foreign ministry "constructive signal" showed up on two different decisions (035-m9.png).
- A French president "Philippe Rousseau" appeared at month 7 (030-m7.png) with no explanation.
- Russian state TV saying "they blinked first" (051-end-seen.png). Not plausible.
- Ranking says "Better than 10% of competent leaders" while the ladder puts 629 below the median 659 (054-end-rank.png).

## End
I reached the end screen: Mission complete, B, 2 stars, 629/1000 (048-end3b.png), ten achievements, a moments timeline, how I was seen, and "The truth" (053-end-truth.png). The truth screen, with my world next to the real one as of Nov 2026, was the best thing in the game for me. The month 12 debrief itself never says what the ceasefire terms were.

## Knowledge
I knew everything it showed. The "Nuclear program 100%" card for Russia and "1221k" without a unit read oddly.
