# Marcus, 2026 Term, laptop-small

I played the 2026 Term as the United States, then started a second Term to test the opposite choice.

**What worked.** Month 1 opened with Sanctions on Iran and a cabinet pick I could accept or override. The debrief gave real cause and effect: "approval 42 to 45% (all your choices together +3, everything else -1)" and "Score 100 to 100... heading to 98 if nothing changes" (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/008-m1d.png). The mission bar moving from -100 toward -70 made progress concrete. Screens fit 1366x768 and all controls were tappable.

**What did not.**
- I wanted to replay the same crisis with a different choice. The second Term opened with Gaza famine, not Iran sanctions (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/032-r2b.png). I found no way to reuse my seed or branch from a month. My first game (month 11) also vanished from Saved games (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/033-menu.png), possibly because the test driver clears storage. That is my quit point: I cannot make a fair comparison, so the what-if tool is not there yet.
- After voting for sanctions the Diplomacy page showed a big FAILED stamp over a UN board reading YES 4, NO 12 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/010-m1f.png). Did my vote fail, or Iran's response? China and Russia "move against you" were also stamped FAILED. That reads as random.
- Month 1 headline was a staff order (National address), not my vote (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/007-m1x.png). Months 2 and 3 said "What your choice did" when I chose nothing (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/012-m2x.png).
- Political capital stayed at "1 left" every month and I never learned how it works.
- My first text tap on a card failed and the page scrolled (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/005-m1b.png).

**Names I did not know.** Mojtaba Khamenei as Supreme Leader, Mokha, IRGC, the Abu Dhabi talks. The "2 names" chip and "What is going on here?" helped when I opened them.

**Plausibility.** The war with Iran dragged on in game one and ended in month 5 in game two (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-02/shots/marcus-term/040-qq3.png), but the openings differed, so I cannot say if that is believable.

**Scores.** Recommend 6, replay 4, realism 6, clarity 6, visual 8.

Biggest wish: replay the same seed with a changed choice and compare side by side.
