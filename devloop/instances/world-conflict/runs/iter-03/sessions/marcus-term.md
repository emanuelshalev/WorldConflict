# Marcus, One Term 1990 (T1990-USA-MARC1), desktop, build d70608a

I played 12 months as Bush (Jan 1990 to Jan 1991). Score 100 to 120, approval 74 to 65. I did not manage the replay-with-opposite-choice test I care about; the game has no obvious way to do it.

**Start.** Play a seed was easy to find, but the label click failed while the globe moved and the box appears below the button (shots/marcus-term/003-seed2.png). The code reproduced the exact opening. I chose "I know this"; the sample sentence about Donetsk is odd in a 1990 game (004-seed3.png).

**Notes before each month.** I expected: answer the crisis, maybe spend capital. Mostly right; I could skip the rest. After month 5 I just pressed Accept all recommended, because it is so easy, and I stopped thinking. Two crises (General strike, Outbreak) were answered by the cabinet pick without my reading them.

**What worked.** The debrief (See everything, Diplomacy page) gives before and after numbers and a leader quote. Era names are right: Kaifu, Roh Tae-woo, Mitterrand, Ozal, Andreotti (030-cur.png).

**Clickable or not.** Tapping an option's heading did nothing; the picture selected it (006-m1b.png). A "free" order cost Approval -3, hidden under "+1 more" (009-m1d.png).

**Map mode (months 3 and 4).** I found the recommended list and Execute, but not the crisis text, only "Crisis answer: Fire them and prosecute" (013-m3map2.png). The list shrinks the globe to a ball.

**Bugs.**
- Quiet-month advisor cards collapse into letter-per-line columns (019-m5.png, S2).
- Mission strip says Iran -75 to -89 while the report says -74 to -69 (014-m3r.png, 015-m3d.png, S2). This is exactly the unexplained outcome I hate.
- UN board shows YES 14 NO 2, sixteen votes on a fifteen-seat council (025-m6e.png, S3).
- Phone calls use an election-projection picture (039-cur.png, S4).

**Plausibility.** Tension 34 to 48 in one month with no stated cause (023-m6.png). A clandestine Iraqi nuclear program, an outbreak with a vaccine effort, and an Iraq nuclear-threshold crisis in Dec 1990 are not 1990. Every leader answers calls with the same sentence; Court Iran came up each month.

**Scores (1-10).** Fun 6, clarity 6, visual 7, game feel 6, agency 6, realism 5, replay 5, recommend 6.

**Quit point.** None.

**Biggest wish.** Let me replay a crisis with the opposite choice and compare, and keep the numbers consistent.
