# Olivia (laptop), iteration 3, build d70608a, control game CA-USA-OLIV1

I played the whole Flashpoint (12 months) because I was never blocked, about 25 minutes. I would not have quit; the closest I came was month 5, when it turned into pressing the same buttons.

**Start.** The title globe is pretty. "Play a seed" took me a moment (my first tap by label missed); the code worked first try and dropped me into Caracas After Maduro as the US (shots/olivia-laptop/003-seed2.png). I picked "Guide me" because I know almost no names. Names I did not know: Machado, Rodriguez, Maduro, Caracas, snapback, "regime change, without the change", Khamenei, Hamas, stabilization force. The underline plus "1 name" chip helped a little.

**Each month, my guess versus truth.** Month 1 I guessed: pick one card, press Execute, ignore everything else. Right. Accept cabinet pick then Execute gave a big "-10 Venezuela's view of you" card with a quote, which is satisfying (007-r1.png). Months 2 to 12: I guessed "Accept all recommended, Execute", right every time. I could skip budgets, meetings, goal text. The note "2 staff proposals not accepted (they will not run)" confused me.

**Map mode (months 2 and 3).** Beautiful globe. Tapping Venezuela opened a clear order menu (009-map2b.png) with chances and a greyed pact that says why. But my "Add to this month" click did not register, I executed with 0 orders, and in month 3 the game flipped back to Desk by itself (013-map3b.png). I could find Execute and the Review recommended button; I could not tell if my order was queued.

**Annoying.** Next month appears twice per month (result card, then the paper). Small grey jargon text (legitimacy, tension, "Hurts: Approval of 55%+") feels like work software.

**End.** Mission complete, A, 3 stars, 746 (028-end.png showed a big 0 first). Badges are fun (030-rv2.png). The moments screen says "The war ended in month 3" and my best moment was "Spy on Iran", in a Venezuela game, which I never understood (035-mo.png). Sidebar stages are locked until seen. The ladder and four buttons at the end made the way back clear; New game took me to the title (043-f3.png).

**Scores:** fun 6, clarity 6, visual 8, game-feel 7, agency 5, realism 6, replay 5, recommend 6.

Quit point: none. Biggest wish: make months 5 to 12 feel different, and let me decide something big myself instead of Accept all.
