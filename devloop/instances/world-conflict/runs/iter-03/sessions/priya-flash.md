# Priya (priya-flash), laptop, seed CO-UKR-PRIY1, build d70608a

I played the whole Flashpoint as Zelensky, reached the end screen (mission complete, 714, A, 3 stars) and started a second game (Hormuz Convoy as the US) from the Ranking screen.

**Start.** The Play a seed field was not obvious: the disabled Play this seed beside Today's crisis looks like the button, and I had to click into the box before typing (shots/priya-flash/004-s2.png). I answered "I know this". The i marks and "What is going on here?" stay on every crisis, which is noise for me.

**Playing.** Each month I guessed: answer the crisis, skip most orders. Right every time. Accept all recommended plus Execute got me through quickly. The goal line with Helps/Hurts tags is the best clarity feature (006-m1.png). I could not tell whether tapping an option selected it (007-m1b.png).

**Map mode (months 4 and 5).** I found the order menu by tapping a country, refusal reasons were clear (Russia rebuffed, needs relations PROFITABLE, 020-map4c.png) and Review recommended / Execute sat in the strip. I did not miss anything.

**Realism.** Good: Kramatorsk zone, US trade offer, corruption scandal, coalition of the willing, Trump, Merz. Bad: one Propose ceasefire order ended the war in month 3 (018-map4.png), after which a Russian ultimatum and generic templates (Energy price shock, Beg for Patriots, Burn their refineries, Grid down) kept coming. Court United Kingdom produced Denmark and Germany reactions rather than the UK's (015-d2.png).

**Bug.** The advisor cards render as one-letter-wide columns (013-m2c.png). S2.

**End sequence.** The count (a 377-seat Rada majority, odd, 039-end1.png), the verdict, achievements (nice), moments (lists "Accept it publicly" under went badly although it carried my mission, 041-r2.png), numbers, and The truth (044-r5.png) which compares my run with the real world. That is the part I came for. Ranking offers Replay same opening, New world, Compare runs (greyed) and New game; I guessed New game and landed on the title. The way back worked, the labels are muddy.

**Scores:** fun 7, clarity 7, visual 7, game feel 7, agency 8, realism 5, replay 7, recommend 7.
