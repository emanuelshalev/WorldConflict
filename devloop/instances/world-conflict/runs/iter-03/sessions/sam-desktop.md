# Sam, desktop, control game SL-CHN-SAM01 (build d70608a)

Verdict in five minutes: crafted game, with seams. Recommend 6/10. No quit point. Screenshots in `devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-03/sessions/sam-desktop/`.

**First impression.** The title globe with a live strip and a TODAY marker reads as a game at once (`001-title.png`). "Play a seed" worked, though the first press failed while the globe turned (`002-seed.png`). The news question was easy: Some help. The little "3 names" chips were quiet, and the Who is who strip was nice (`004-seed3.png`). I did not open an explanation, so I cannot judge their text.

**Month by month, my guess before I pressed.** Month 1: pick one crisis answer, skip the rest. Right. Month 2: same, plus try Map. Right. After that I guessed "Accept all, Execute" and was right every month, which is the problem: from month 3 I made no decisions, political capital sat at 6 of 6 and I spent one point all game.

**What felt authored.** The ACCEPTED and ORDERED stamps (`008-m1c.png`, `016-map2e.png`), the dispatch card with a big number and mission bar (`009-e1.png`), the verdict stamp (`045-verdict.png`), and the reality table with sources (`043-r5.png`). Like the end of Suzerain, but shorter.

**Craft defects, with places.**
- Month 2, "Your advisors suggest": the cards collapse, titles wrap one letter per line, buttons float over art (`011-m2b.png`). Worst thing I saw. Frostpunk would never ship that.
- Right rail: "Accept all recommended" sits on top of the second recommendation (`010-m2.png`). The desk also keeps last month's scroll, so the hero art is cut off.
- Crisis options reuse one scene three times, the middle card sits 4px high, a pin icon pokes out of the Cabinet pick tag (`005-m1.png`, `021-m4.png`). Into the Breach gives each choice its own face.
- Dispatch says "Jinping's answer" (Xi is the surname) and uses three verbs for one card; Economy -65 has no unit and a stamp covers the label (`020-exec3.png`).
- Relations are Hostile, lamentable and LAMENTABLE (-56) in one panel (`015-map2d.png`). Vocabulary is my deal-breaker.
- German flag in month 11 is red and gold stripes (`035-n10.png`).
- Reveal counts 13 orders (I gave one) and shows "377 seats, majority won" for a one-party state (`038-x12.png`). The verdict says 5 of 5 goals while the desk said four.

**Map mode.** Two months. The globe, layer switch and bottom strip are good and I found everything: dossier, orders, Review recommended, Execute. But three taps on Taiwan and the coast did nothing until I hit the marker (`013-map2b.png`), and the verbs differ from the desk (Accept order / Add / Remove vs Accept). Same result, different words: S2 only half passes.

**Realism.** Months 3 to 5 were Afghan refugees, Iran sanctions and Gaza on a Scarborough game. Filler.

**Way back.** From the end screen, "New game" returned to the title with Continue and Replay same opening: clear. I did not start a second game; I had my answer.

**Scores:** fun 6, clarity 7, visual 7, game-feel 7, agency 7, realism 6, replay 5, recommend 6.

**Biggest wish:** make the middle of the game ask me something, and fix the advisor cards.
