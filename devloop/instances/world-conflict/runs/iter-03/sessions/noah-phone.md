Played all 12 months in about 25 minutes, then the verdict and a second game start.

**Start.** Play a seed worked first try, under a minute. I picked Guide me. The example sentence there was about Donetsk, not my game (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-03/shots/noah-phone/004-seed3.png). Names like Al Dhafra, Fujairah, Khamenei, IRGC, Abiy Ahmed meant nothing to me. The little i chips explain them but I would not tap them.
