4. **Skip is opaque**: the default deputy answer and the "Xi notices" penalty are hidden (dan, marcus, priya want numbers; maya, olivia say the dashed box looks disabled; skip wording varies per item).
5. **Eight items a month is heavy** on a phone or when you know the game (maya, noah, leo "homework", dan, marcus, sam). Experts want Full desk.
6. **A and B are indistinguishable on the 390 phone shots** (maya, noah): the variant comparison is not valid for phone.
7. **Approve all on the map invites rubber-stamping** (dan, marcus), though olivia loves it, and the map bar is overloaded (sam, noah, leo "dead map").
