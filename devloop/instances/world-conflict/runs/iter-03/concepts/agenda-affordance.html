One look for what you can press

Everything pressable is raised, outlined and has a visible cue. Everything else is flat and has no border or pill. A test checks it on every screen.

Clickable

Raised (bottom shadow), outlined, sinks 1px when pressed, focus ring, at least 44 px tall on touch.

Choice cardRadio cue on the right; selected = amber border and filled radio.
LinkBlue and always underlined (not only on hover).
SwitchA bordered pill is always a switch or a chip you can press.

Not clickable

Flat: no border, no shadow, no cue, no hover change, normal cursor.
