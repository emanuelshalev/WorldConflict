# Sam, tablet (1024x768)

First verdict: this is a crafted game, not an AI dashboard. The title globe, the serif headlines, the lit-room scene art and the newspaper have a point of view. I played all 12 months of Hormuz Convoy as the US and went through the full reveal.

## What I liked
- Newspaper front page and desk scene art (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/011-paper.png, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/003-desk.png).
- YOUR PICK stamp on a chosen card (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/005-card.png).
- Count-up verdict with stamp, then achievement badges with rarity (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/027-verdict2.png, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/028-ach.png).

## Craft defects (tablet)
- Title: the headline and Today card sit on top of map labels and pins; seed box covers Africa (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/001-title.png). Compare Frostpunk's title, which clears the scene.
- Coach marks cover what they explain and the second arrow points at nothing (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/005-card.png).
- Desk cards: card 3 is lower than 1 and 2, its tag hides the free badge, the stamp covers the Hurts text.
- Right rail truncates order names (Reply via Mu...) and clips the list behind Approve (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/003-desk.png).
- Map mode: staff chip text overlaps the Approve button (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/006-map.png).
- Month result card pushes its buttons below the fold; colours disagree (green number, red chip for a drop) (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/009-exec.png).

## Consistency (S2)
Order cards match between desk and map, good. But Political capital on desk and HUD becomes a diamond count on the map. My Add tap on the Mexico menu did not queue (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/008-added.png). Mexico "won't sign a treaty in the middle of a war" made no sense (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/007-mexico.png).

## Truth problems
Newspaper shows a US situation room for a North Korea story, masthead says month 1 while HUD says 2. The end screen says MISSION FAILED, grade D, yet Master Diplomat. Approval start 50 in the chart vs 44 in the briefing (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/sam-tablet/030-e4.png).

## Names I did not know
IRGC, Muscat, Mojtaba Khamenei, defence pacts, midterms context. No in-place explanation.

## Scores
Fun 6, clarity 6, visual 7, game-feel 7, agency 6, realism 5, replay 6, recommend 6. No quit point; I would keep going to see the reveal.
