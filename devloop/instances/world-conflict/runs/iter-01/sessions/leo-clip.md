# Leo, phone clip run (Hormuz Convoy, US)

I picked the globe pin marked TODAY, open war, and hit "Take office". Fast start, good (shots/leo-clip/002-start.png).

**Month 1.** The loudest choice, "Hit the IRGC navy now". Tapping the card text did nothing for a bit; the label button worked, then a YOUR PICK stamp showed (008-pick3.png). IRGC, Muscat, Isfahan's tunnels: no clue what these are.

**Chaos.** I opened Full desk, All orders. Typing "nuke" fired hotkeys instead of searching (016-nuke.png). "Pick a country" buttons do nothing; the dropdown only worked with arrow keys, painful on a phone. Then Declare war on Australia: the red "There is no undo, allies will join" card is great (025-o10.png), and the WAR DECLARED stinger is the best clip moment of the run (burst-027-war/000.png). The newspaper after it is lovely (030-m4b.png). Nuclear strike was greyed with "Only in wartime", a clear showable reason. I never got to a nuke or a coup.

**Middle.** Months 4 to 10 are the same room image and same bars. Dead on camera. The war headline even said "The war with Iran is over" after I attacked Australia (028-war.png). Political capital piled up to 6 unused.

**End (month 12).** "See your verdict" gave a STEP ACHIEVED stamp, then a sequence: MISSION FAILED, grade D, "Reckless Adventurer" (064-v1.png), achievements (Peacemaker on a war run, odd), The Moments timeline with "Declare war on Australia" as best moment (067-v3.png), ranking ladder "better than 15%" (071-v7.png), the fog-lifts reveal (070-v6.png), and a share card with seed (073-share.png). Phone card and Save card gave no feedback (074-phonecard.png).

**Verdict.** The end is worth posting. The middle needs more spectacle, and wild moves need a faster path.

Scores: fun 7, clarity 6, visual 7, game-feel 7, agency 6, realism 6, replay 7, recommend 7. Quit point: none.
