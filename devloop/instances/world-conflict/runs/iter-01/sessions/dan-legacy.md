# Dan, dan-legacy (laptop 1440x900, Legacy 2026, Russia, Hard), build e0e42bc

I played 12 of 120 months, mostly with Approve and Execute, and read every result table. Systems probing was only partly possible.

## What I liked
- **The month table** (shots/dan-legacy/021-tbl.png, 041-chip.png): every number has a from and to, a reply and a watch line. This is the thing I asked for in round 3.
- **Score explainer** (shots/dan-legacy/009-score.png): four parts, 15% drift toward what the situation earns, and a tooltip on the HUD saying why it moved. Approval shows "your choices +6, everything else -3".
- **Map order panel** (shots/dan-legacy/032-pol.png): success chances (Spy 95%, Destabilize 76%) and a plain refusal reason: Poland won't sign a defence pact mid-war. That is the rule shown that I wanted.
- **World drawer** (shots/dan-legacy/042-world.png): fronts, exhaustion, pacts with strength.

## What bothered me
- **Nothing to optimise yet.** The Legacy mission is "Survive to the end of your term" (005-brief.png). Same complaint as round 1, 120 months long.
- **Staff pre-spent my capital** (006-desk.png). I got to month 6 with the advisor re-adding "Mobilize forces" each month: mobilization 100 to 100, stability 57 to 55, "your 6th order of this kind" (041-chip.png). That is fake depth, and it looks like an exploit against the player.
- **No sizes before I commit** (016-dem.png). Cards say "Approval up" and "+1 more", not by how much. The numbers only come after Execute.
- **Forecast versus result.** I demanded a full withdrawal from Zelensky: the card said tension up, the result was "listens, promises nothing", tension 74 to 73 (021-tbl.png). Courting Poland made its view of me drop -80 to -92. No rule shown for either.
- **Country picker** (013-iran.png, 014-ukr.png): a native dropdown with unexplained bracket numbers, and typing did not select. I abandoned it after two blocks and used the Call meeting instead.
- **Layout**: the cabinet-pick pill overlaps the minister's quote on a Call (015-call.png); the map forecast is clipped by the bottom bar (033-fc.png).
- **Names**: Camille Girard (France) I did not know. "Seals", "exhaustion 51%", "Front 55/100", "Intelligence on Turkey 73.8 to 95", "Reassure: no threat intended" and the dots on the nation list have no explanation.

## Verdict
This is the most transparent game of this kind I have tried, and that is rare. But after 12 months I cannot say whether the depth is real or whether one move (do nothing, approve everything) is fine. The score slid from 100 to 85 on its own, which suggests passivity loses slowly, not that anything wins. I did not quit; I would play Term to see the finale, and Legacy only if it gets goals.

Scores: recommend 6, clarity 8, agency 5.
