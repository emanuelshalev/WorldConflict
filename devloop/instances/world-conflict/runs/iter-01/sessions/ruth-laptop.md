# Ruth, laptop (1440x900; 125% zoom could not be applied, noted where relevant)

I picked the Ukraine one because I read about the talks this morning, and took the American side. The title screen (shots/ruth-laptop/001-title.png) is striking, but the globe sits over the text on the left and I could not read half of the blurb (004-pick3.png). Seeds and "Play this seed" meant nothing to me.

The mission screen was the best moment: five plain goals, "on track" or "off track" (006-start.png). I knew the leaders. I liked that each order card says what it helps and hurts in ordinary words (008-desk.png), and the Chronicle front page (014-paper.png).

Then the desk. It is a lot: seals, budget, meetings, advisors, orders. In month 2 the staff had already used my political capital on things I never asked for, so I had nothing left. I wanted to propose a ceasefire to Russia, but "Pick a country" opens a plain dropdown of 41 names with numbers in brackets (020-sel2.png). I could not manage it. The map was no better: it starts on America, Russia is off the edge, and I do not drag (024-mapz.png). That is two "what do I press" moments, nearly three. I took the advisor suggestion instead, and I think it did not even register (my Add click failed).

The news confused me. After my Ukraine move, the result card said "Your forces gain ground against Iran" and later "The war with Iran is over" (026-r2.png, 028-r3.png). I did not know America was at war with Iran in this game. A red "FAILED" stamp on a West Bank card made me think my order failed.

The Poland crisis in month 7 felt like real news (037-d8.png). The art sometimes did not match the story (candles for a weapons decision).

The ending: the "truth" page put my world next to the real November 2026 with sources, exactly what I wanted (056-e5.png). Though it also showed Iran's nuclear percentages and North Korean operations, which I could not follow. Then the verdict said "MISSION FAILED", grade D, no stars, "Master Diplomat", and "better than 34%" beside "top 66%" (058-e7.png). I met four goals of five. I do not know whether I won.

Never played a Term, so R2 is untried. No timers, which I liked.

Recommend: 6. Biggest wish: let me pick a country by tapping its name, and keep the news on my chosen war.
