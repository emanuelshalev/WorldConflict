# Olivia, phone (390x844), build e0e42bc

I clicked a friend's link and got a spinning globe with glowing pins. Pretty, and I knew what to tap (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/001-title.png). I took office as the United States.

**Briefing:** a list of seven goals with percentages and "needs 56%". That is homework. I skimmed and hit Take office (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/002-start.png). Names I do not know: IRGC, midterms clock, Muscat, Bandar Abbas, Qatari text, Gaza order, contributors. The game never explained them (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/003-desk.png, devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/010-m3.png).

**First choice** in about 40 seconds: swipe cards with a picture, a red or green "Hurts/Helps" line and a "Best for your mission" tag. I picked "More escorts, no strikes" and a "YOUR PICK" stamp slammed on (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/006-pick2.png). Lovely. Execute month gave me a card with bars moving and a newspaper line (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/007-exec.png). Month 2 ended with "The war with Iran is over" (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/010-m3.png). That is a real payoff, though it came too easily, and I did not feel I did something clever on purpose.

I kept playing six months. The "STEP ACHIEVED" stamp (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/014-e3.png) made me smile. Honestly I mostly picked whatever was tagged best, which is easy but is not really me being in control.

**What bugged me:** the bottom bar "1 left · 2 decided" and the coins. I had up to 4 coins banked by month 7 and never knew what to buy (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/olivia-phone/021-m7.png). I wanted to "make peace" myself (my second goal) and never found a button for it; the cards decided what I could do. The "Hurts: Approval of 56%+ after the midterms" lines are goal-speak. One stamp said achieved while a number dropped.

**Never blocked, never stupid-feeling,** no page errors. I would not have quit in the first two minutes. I would have drifted away around month 7 when the coins piled up with nothing to do.
