# Noah, tablet portrait (820x1180), Hormuz Convoy as the US

I was in the game within about 30 seconds: the globe with a glowing TODAY pin (shots/noah-tablet/001-title.png) is the best front door I have seen on a strategy game. Small snag: after tapping the pin the start panel was way down the page under the seed box, so I scrolled (shots/noah-tablet/003-dossier2.png). I did not read the mission card, just hit Take office (shots/noah-tablet/004-start.png). IRGC, Muscat, uranium and midterms meant nothing to me.

First turn: three big cards, I picked "Hit the IRGC navy now" and got a YOUR PICK stamp (shots/noah-tablet/007-pick2.png). Execute, and a result card with approval and tension bars (shots/noah-tablet/008-exec1.png). Good, but a lot of text. Month 2 "Send in special forces" gave a jet strike scene (shots/noah-tablet/011-m2r.png), my best moment. After that I mostly pressed Execute on whatever the cabinet suggested, because it was faster. That is a risk: the game plays itself, and advisors pre-add orders I never chose (a sabotage that failed and later appeared as a bad moment, shots/noah-tablet/038-r3.png).

Newspaper in month 6 said the war is over (shots/noah-tablet/021-m7r.png), nice payoff, though it said month 6 while the top bar said 7.

End: verdict stamp MISSION FAILED, grade D, 0 stars, yet "Master Diplomat" and 663/1000 (shots/noah-tablet/036-verdict2.png). I missed by one point on Israel (60 vs 61). Felt unfair and confusing. The badges screen is great (shots/noah-tablet/037-r2.png), moments timeline is fine, "The fog lifts" with my guess crossed out vs the truth is the one real thing I could repeat in class (shots/noah-tablet/041-r6.png). Numbers screen says 0 deals though I signed one (shots/noah-tablet/040-r5.png). Ranking has a share card and Share result, Save card, Replay same opening (shots/noah-tablet/042-r7.png). The card says failed, D; I would not post that.

Would I have quit? No, the pace kept me, but the last 4 months were all taps with no thinking.

Scores: fun 6, clarity 5, visual 8, game feel 7, agency 6, realism 7, replay 6, recommend 6.

Biggest wish: let me make one huge move each month that clearly changes the screen, and make the end card something I want to post even when I lose.
