# Priya, tablet portrait, Ceasefire or Collapse as Ukraine

I played all 12 months in a lecture break, about 35 minutes. I picked Ukraine and read the cards.

**What worked.** The title globe is clear (shots/priya-tablet/001-title.png). Each card says what it hurts and helps, and who backs it (007-desk.png). That met my need to understand a choice before I take it. The Approved stamp is a nice touch (013-picked.png). The best part by far was "The truth" (047-truth.png, 048-truth2.png): ISW numbers, the Abu Dhabi talks of 10-11 November, the 28, 19 and 20 point plans, with sources. That is the real-world debrief I wanted.

**What did not.** The dossier says "two days before the midterms" on 22 November 2026, but the midterms were on 3 November (004-happening.png). That is a factual slip and I noticed at once. "Prime Minister Andy Burnham" I could not confirm, so I flagged it.

After month 1 the story stopped being Ukraine. I got a cyberattack, a general strike, an energy shock, a corruption scandal and "the streets are boiling" (015-m3.png, 019-mm4.png). There were no talks about territory, guarantees or the coalition of the willing, though that is my mission. The war just "ended" in month 6 and the paper gave no reason, only "Zelensky answered Energy price shock with Subsidize fuel" (024-res7.png). The reaction quote, "Crowds gather outside the embassy of Ukraine in the United States", was attributed to Trump and then to Putin (010-m1.png, 014-r2.png). It reads like a template.

Smaller things: US view fell 45 to 36 and the bar was green (010-m1.png). Score "needs 82" sits next to a 1000-point verdict. A ghost panel overlaps the dossier on tablet. The desk opened scrolled down after Next month. The paper sometimes needed a second Next month press.

**The ending.** MISSION FAILED, grade D, "better than 0% of competent leaders" (049-rank.png), though the war ended and four of five goals were met. The moments screen lists approval 67 to 73 under "went badly", and the timeline labels overlap (044-moments.png). I did not trust that grade.

**Scores.** Fun 6, clarity 7, visual 8, game feel 6, agency 7, realism 5, replay 5, recommend 6.

I would not have quit, but I would have stopped caring around month 4 without the debrief to look forward to.
