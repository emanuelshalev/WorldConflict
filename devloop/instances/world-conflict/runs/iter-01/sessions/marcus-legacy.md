# Marcus, marcus-legacy (laptop, 1990 Legacy as the United States)

Played about 45 minutes, 14 of 120 months, build e0e42bc.

**First impression.** The title globe is handsome and I started a 1990 Legacy with no trouble. The briefing card (George H. W. Bush, "Stay in power") felt like a proper opening (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/005-start.png). One slip: after picking 1990 the header still said 22 Nov 2026, tension 74 (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/003-era1990.png). The Russia dossier described modern Russia, not the Soviet Union, and Ukraine was already its own country.

**What I liked.** The monthly result card says in a sentence what my choice did, with before and after numbers (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/007-m1.png). Orders show a forecast and a chance before I commit (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/025-court.png). The map uses 1990 names (West Germany) and the leaders are right: Thatcher, Kohl, Kaifu, Mitterrand, Hawke, Jiang, Ozal, Kim Il Sung.

**Where it lost me.**
- Numbers disagree. Approval was 70%, 71.1 and 71 on three cards; score 103 on one page, 104 on the next (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/009-all.png). A man who checks cause and effect notices at once.
- In June the tension went 33 to 47, shown under "Court North Korea" (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/027-d6.png). The real cause, a Pakistani nuclear test in the ticker, was not linked. The table said 42.6 while the HUD said 47.
- The Foreign Minister suggests "Court North Korea" every month. Relations snap back to -99 and the same card repeats (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/032-res7.png). I pressed Approve and nothing pushed back.
- Realism. The Chronicle for Nov 1990 had Ukraine and Denmark signing a military pact, Salinas re-elected, a cyber "Grid down" and an "Outbreak" crisis, and no Gulf crisis at all (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/037-chron.png). This is the 1990 campaign I came for and the world has left the real one. For a what-if tool that is the deal breaker.
- The orders menu target is a plain dropdown that ignored my typing; I only managed orders through the map (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/017-orders.png).
- The Foreign Minister quote is squeezed into a one-word-per-line column in places (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/037-chron.png).

**Words I did not know** (the game did not help): Hot zone, Hunger meter, Legitimacy, "Lamentable -81" for Cuba, a "FAILED" stamp on Gaza with no explanation (devloop/instances/world-conflict/runs/iter-01/shots/marcus-legacy/011-home.png), and a few Africa and Southeast Asia names.

**Replay.** I looked for a way to rerun a month with the opposite choice and found none. That was my first goal.

**Scores** (1-10): fun 6, clarity 6, visual 8, feels like a game 7, agency 6, realism 4, replay 4, recommend 5.

**Quit point.** None, but I would stop at the first anachronism if this were my own weekend.
