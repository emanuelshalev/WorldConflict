# Concept test results (iteration 1): learning aids (G12) and world map (G11)

Panel v1.2, 9 personas, blind, screenshots only. Weighted 100-point splits.

## Learning aids
| | A underline + peek | B briefing panel | C quiet "i" + "What is going on here?" |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted points | 31.9 | 26.2 | **41.8** |
| Preferred by | Maya, Noah | Dan, Sam, Marcus | Olivia, Ruth, Leo, Priya |

Familiarity level they would pick (weighted): Guide me 26%, Some help 45%, I know this 29%. The setting is needed: no single level fits.

**Patterns**
- **A's popover covers the Execute button and the option cards** (Sam, Priya, Marcus, Olivia, Ruth): a deal-breaker as drawn. Hover-only is a deal-breaker on tablets (Ruth).
- **The tiny "i" is too small and icon-only** (Ruth, Olivia); the big labelled "What is going on here?" button saves C.
- **Universally disliked:** the codex unlock toast (8 of 9), auto-opening "Brief me on each new crisis" (6 of 9), underlines for experts (Dan, Sam, Priya, Marcus, Leo).
- **Phone casuals want tap-and-peek** (Maya, Noah: one line, then back to playing), not a long briefing before choosing.
- Unknown terms flagged by the knowledge-adjusted personas: Donetsk oblast, fortress belt, Kramatorsk, Sloviansk, Pokrovsk axis, the Rada, Oleshky, demilitarising, Mojtaba Khamenei as Supreme Leader, the midterms. RS-003 confirmed: even news followers (Ruth, Marcus) meet unfamiliar names.
