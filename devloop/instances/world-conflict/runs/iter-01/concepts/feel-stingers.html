Big moments

Five moments get a full-screen stinger: war declared, nuclear use, coup, peace, mission complete. Each runs 2.4 seconds, is skippable with any tap or key from the first frame, plays at most once per month (highest priority wins, the others become the debrief highlight), and ends by shrinking into the card that tells the story, so it never strands the player. Built from the house scenes, the existing stamp and slam motions, and the synthesized sound kit.
