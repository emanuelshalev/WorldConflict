Pips mean the same thing everywhere: a filled diamond is capital you can still spend, a hatched one is held by an order in this month (frees if you remove it), a dotted one is next month's refill. The HUD and the desk rail draw this one meter, so "◆ 1 left" never shows as filled on one and empty on the other. The pulse runs twice (1.6 s), then a static ring stays while the reason is on screen. Map menu rules: the six most relevant orders for the target, ranked by the shared registry (ui/src/lib/orders.ts), then "All 19 orders for India", which expands in place to the exact list the desk shows for India. Tapping an available card queues it and the menu stays open: the card stamps, the header ◆ ticks down, the HUD meter pulses once. Hover (desktop) or the card's info button (touch) opens the same preview panel the desk shows: summary, effects, risks, mission warning.

At 1024 the menu is the same width (380px) and the reason lines wrap to two lines. At 390 the menu becomes a bottom sheet of rows (next section).
