The political capital seal

Political capital is one physical thing: a wax seal with an embossed diamond. You get 2 a month (3 in a Flashpoint), you can hold 6, and every order that costs capital carries its seals. One rule everywhere: a full seal is one you can still spend. A dashed imprint is a seal already placed on something this month. The word is always "political capital" (never "credit", "capital points" or "Cap").

Anatomy and sizes

  • Wax blob: 16-lobe edge, radial gradient from #ffe2a3 to #8d5512 (light from top left, the same key light as the scenes).
  • Ring: pressed ring at 61% radius, darker stroke plus a 0.7px highlight offset up-left (the emboss).
  • Diamond: the ◆ glyph the game already uses, pressed in (#9a5c14) with a lit edge, so text "◆2" and the seal read as the same thing.
  • One SVG symbol, five variants: full, imprint, short, broken (spent at Execute), new (minted). Component: <CapitalSeals left committed short size />.
