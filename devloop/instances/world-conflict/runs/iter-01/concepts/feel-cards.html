Briefing cards

Every decision option and every order is a physical card: an illustration band from the scene kind it will play as, a title, effect chips with arrows and words, its cost in capital seals, and the person who backs it. Cards lift on hover, sink on press, and are stamped when chosen. A card that cannot be played says why on its face.

States (crisis option and order cards share one component)

1. Rest no motion
Paper surface gradient, 1px edge, 4px "thickness" shadow below. Illustration slightly dimmed so the chosen card can brighten.
2. Hover or focus 140 ms ease-out
Lifts 4px, shadow grows, illustration to full colour. Keyboard focus looks the same plus the 2px focus ring. No sound on hover.
3. Pressed 80 ms, "tap"
Sinks 2px and to 98.5%, thickness collapses to 1px. Starts on pointerdown, so feedback is under 50 ms even if the server preview is slow.
4. Chosen: "Your call" 280 ms overshoot, "stamp"
Stamp lands from 180% and -16 degrees. Accent border 2px, warm glow. Sibling cards drop to 78% opacity (never lower: they stay readable).
5. Staff pick approved same stamp, steel ink
"Approve staff picks" stamps every pre-filled card in sequence, 90 ms apart, one stamp sound per card (max 4 sounds, then silent).
6. Cannot be played reason on the card
Never only a tooltip. The empty seal hole in the cost shows what is missing. "Show me" pulses the bank (and on the map menu, the same words).
7. Refused on press 260 ms shake, "refuse"
Pressing anyway: the card shakes, the bank shakes in sync and its missing seals flash red twice. No toast that outlives the moment.
8. Queued order 420 ms flight to the plan
The card's mini flies to the plan (FLIP); its seal flies from the bank onto it. A dashed ghost stays here with Undo, so the layout never jumps.
