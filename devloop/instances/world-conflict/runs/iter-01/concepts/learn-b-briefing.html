The free economic zone

Staff pick: Only by referendum✓ Approve staff pick💬 Consult your staff

US mediators propose demilitarising the unoccupied part of Donetsk oblast, including the fortress belt of Kramatorsk and Sloviansk, as an internationally run economic zone. Moscow calls it 'a basis'. Your generals call it surrender by spreadsheet.

Washington wants an answer before the midterms. Accepting in principle keeps the Americans close but hands Moscow the fortress belt without a shot. Demanding a referendum first is constitutionally correct and politically safe, and the White House will read it as a no. Rejecting outright and attacking shows the world Ukraine can still fight, at a cost in men you are running short of.

Accept in principle
Keep Washington on side. The street, the army and the Rada will not forgive you quickly.
